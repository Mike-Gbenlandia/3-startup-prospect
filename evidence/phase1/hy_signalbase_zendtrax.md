Zendtrax has raised $50K in pre-seed funding from MARL. The startup is building logistics and supply chain tools aimed at the African e-commerce market, where it says moving goods has become fragmented and exploitative.

Zendtrax's pitch centers on trust as the missing piece in logistics, with products designed to give individuals and business owners more transparency and more options for third-party logistics services. The company says it is also developing additional product verticals for both individual users and businesses.

MARL's 14th cohort: Solarcorp, Bravoo, Zendtrax — $50K each, $150K combined, demo day Nov 12

US-based venture firm MARL has picked three Nigerian startups for the 14th cohort of its global accelerator, with $50,000 in funding for each and $150,000 in pre-seed capital combined.

The three: Solarcorp, a climate-tech company replacing diesel generators in shopping malls and commercial clusters with shared solar-and-storage systems. Bravoo, a consumer platform built around rewards and gamified experiences. Zendtrax, enterprise software for procurement, inventory and supply chains.
