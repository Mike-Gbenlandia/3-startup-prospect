**The United Nations Industrial Development Organisation (UNIDO) and BFA Global have selected nine climate adaptation startups from eight African countries for the first cohort of the Africa Ignite programme.**

Funded by the Adaptation Fund Climate Innovation Accelerator (AFCIA) and run under the Adaptation SME Innovation Facility (ASIF), Ignite targets early-stage startups that have built a working prototype but are yet to validate it with users. The nine were chosen from 453 applications.

Over eight weeks, founders will test their solutions with farmers, cooperatives and agribusinesses. Weekly live sessions, specialist office hours, AI training and real-world user testing will help them gather evidence of demand and impact, sharpen their business models, and prepare to land customers and raise investment.

“Every founder in this cohort has a working prototype. The next step is often the hardest: putting it in front of a cassava farmer, a coffee cooperative or a poultry producer and learning what they will use and pay for,” said Rasima Swarup, ASIF lead at BFA Global.

The cohort includes Benin’s ALLO VETO, which helps poultry farmers formulate affordable feed from local ingredients when drought disrupts supply; Angolan biodigester maker EcoBioGás; and Tanzania’s GeoVLUP Adaptation Services, which turns farm and climate data into practical guidance for coffee farmers.

Also selected are Ugandan circular savings platform Ghala Circular Solutions; Zimbabwe’s Global Vision Impact, which captures and stores runoff water for households during dry spells; and Kenyan agritech Kilimora, whose AgriKonnekt platform delivers localised climate intelligence to cooperatives.

Rounding out the cohort are Zambia’s LinkBridge, which builds shared solar-powered irrigation for clusters of smallholder farmers; Malawian food traceability startup MTENGO wa Mizu; and Kenya’s North Coast Innovators, which cuts post-harvest losses for cassava farmers through solar drying and milling.

More than half of the startups are founded or co-founded by women, and seven of the nine have a woman in a leadership position.

The programme kicked off on November 11, pauses in December and resumes in January, with the startups set to present their results at a Founder Showcase and Feedback Forum in late February 2027.
