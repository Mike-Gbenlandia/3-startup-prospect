## Questions about Software startup funding in Nigeria, 2026

How many funding rounds did Software startups in Nigeria raise in 2026?StartupList Africa tracks 6 rounds in this view as of 25 Nov 2026, listed newest first.

How often is funding data updated?Our funding database is updated daily. We track disclosed rounds from press releases, regulatory filings, investor announcements, and verified startup submissions.

What funding stages are tracked?We track all stages including Pre-Seed, Seed, Series A through Series E+, as well as alternative capital types like angel investments, grants, debt financing, and convertible notes.

How is the funding amount determined?Amounts shown are the disclosed USD equivalent at the time of announcement. Where the round was denominated in local currency, we convert using the exchange rate on the announcement date.
