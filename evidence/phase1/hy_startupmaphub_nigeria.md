## Seed funding for African startups: common questions

Everything founders ask before they create an account.

### What is seed funding for African startups?

Seed funding helps early-stage African startups build MVPs, hire early team members, run pilots, and reach initial traction.

### What counts as seed funding on this page?

On this page, seed funding includes equity-free grants, accelerator capital, angel investment, and early-stage venture funding available in 2026.

### How do I know which seed funding fits my startup?

Create an account and we match opportunities to your country, sector, and stage automatically — no manual filtering through eligibility rules and deadlines required.

### Do you just show me funding, or help me apply?

Both. We match you to the funds actually open to you, draft every application answer from your own deck, check the deck against the fund's own criteria, and connect you to the events where those investors show up.

### Is it really free to create an account?

Yes. Creating an account is free and gets you your matched list immediately, plus one full AI-drafted application, with no card required. Paid packages exist for founders who want more drafts, deck reviews, and hands-on support — see the pricing page for details.

### How often is the funding list updated?

Weekly. New grants, accelerators, and investor calls are added and stale or closed listings are removed, so the deadlines and eligibility you see are current, not scraped once and forgotten.

### Do you cover accelerators and investors, or just grants?

All four funding types: equity-free grants, accelerator cohorts, angel and VC investors, and debt financing — filtered the same way, by country, sector, and stage.

### What happens after I submit an application?

Nothing on our end delays you — you submit directly to the fund. We track the deadline and status on your account so you know what's outstanding, and we surface matched events where that fund's investors are likely to show up.
