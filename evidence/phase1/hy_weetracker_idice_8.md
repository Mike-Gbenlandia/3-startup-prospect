##### 8 Nigerian Startups Get USD 800 K Through iDICE Growth Lab

GoDash, Telios, Vantrace, KrosAI, Leaflet, Lenol, 360gadgetsafrica, and SavvyBee have been selected for the Federal Government’s iDICE Startup Bridge Growth Lab, receiving USD 100 K each in equity funding.

The eight Nigerian startups span logistics, energy, regulatory technology, artificial intelligence, agriculture, mobility, e-commerce, and fintech.

The Growth Lab, implemented by the Bank of Industry (BOI) and co-financed by the African Development Bank, Agence Française de Développement, and Islamic Development Bank, will provide structured business development support, industry expertise, and investment readiness assistance.

The programme aims to strengthen their operations, address growth constraints, attract additional capital, and support expansion across Nigeria’s digital and creative economy.
