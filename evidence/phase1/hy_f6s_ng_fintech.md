Vittas (Techstars '22) is a Digital Lender using machine-learning/partnerships to provide loans. We built an inventory financing solution for Nigerian healthcare providers that allows them to purchase medications and medical equipment. Healthcare is an underserved segment in an underserved Nigerian market. Our solution allows this sector to access affordable financing. We are also providing accounting/inventory software and POS terminals through two partners to our customers to increase our revenue while also getting access to this data to improve our risk assessment.
