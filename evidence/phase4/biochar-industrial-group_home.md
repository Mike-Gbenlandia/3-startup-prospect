What We Do

## From waste pile to paid product

BIG handles collection, production, carbon credit verification, sales, and revenue sharing.

01

01 — Offtake

### We collect your waste

Palm kernel shells, cashew husks, sawdust. We partner with processors generating 1,000+ tonnes a year. If that's you, you're exactly who we're looking for.

02

02 — Production

### We process it into biochar

Our equipment converts your residues into stable biochar through a controlled heating process. No open burning. No smoke. A clean, high-value output.

03

03 — Payout

### We share carbon credit revenue

Biochar locks carbon out of the atmosphere permanently. That earns verified carbon credits. We sell those credits to international buyers and pay you a share of what we earn.

## Questions processors ask us

What's the partnership model — what does BIG actually do?

BIG (Biochar Industrial Group) is incorporated in the USA. We put our own capital and equipment into your site, financing, installing, and operating an industrial pyrolyzer at no upfront cost to you. Because we're investing rather than selling, we partner selectively.

Revenue from carbon credit sales is split: **20% to you**, 60% to the financier of the machine, and 20% to BIG. If you choose to co-invest in the machine yourself, your share scales proportionally, up to 80%.

Do you partner with every processor who applies?

No. We finance the machine and carry the project, so we partner selectively, the way an investor backs a project. We review every processor against feedstock type, annual volume, and the carbon economics of the site.

Being selected means your feedstock cleared a real bar. We take on a limited number of partners at a time so each site gets the capital and attention it needs to perform.

How much can I actually earn per year?

It depends on your feedstock type and annual volume. [See the calculator above](https://industrialbiochar.com/#calculator) for a live estimate — pick your feedstock and enter your tonnes per year.

For context: a processor running ~5,000 tonnes/year of cashew shells earns roughly $71,000/year at the 0% upfront tier, or $177,000/year at the 50% co-invest tier.

What technology do you use for biochar production?

Continuous-feed industrial pyrolyzers operating at **450–600°C in a low-oxygen environment**. Zero open flame, no smoke.

We've been running this exact system at a sister palm kernel processing facility in Nigeria since 2024 — it's proven, not prototype.

What are your feedstock specifications and moisture requirements?

Minimum volume: **1,000 tonnes/year**. Moisture thresholds vary by feedstock:

- **Palm kernel shells** — moisture below 15%
- **Cashew nut shells** — moisture below 15%
- **Sawdust** — moisture below 20%

If your material runs wetter than this, we can usually accommodate it with a short pre-drying step, talk to us.

What if my volume isn't there yet, or I'm not selected?

You still leave with something useful. Use the [calculator](https://industrialbiochar.com/#calculator) to see what your waste is worth, and we keep your details on file as our pipeline opens up and as we build toward aggregating smaller sites.

Know another processor with the right volume? We pay a **$3,000 referral fee** for introductions that turn into deployments — see [how referrals work](https://industrialbiochar.com/#faq-refer) below.

How do I refer a processor — and what do I earn?

We pay a flat **$3,000 referral fee** for every processor you introduce that turns into a live deployment. To refer someone:

- Send us their details through the [contact form](https://industrialbiochar.com/#contact) — include their name and how to reach them — or make a direct introduction by email.
- Tell us you referred them so we credit the introduction to you.

If **more than one person** helped make the same introduction, the $3,000 can be **split between them** — just let us know how to divide it. The fee is paid once the referred site goes live.

What's the carbon credit methodology and certification standard?

We use the **European Biochar Certificate (EBC)** and **Rainbow** methodology. MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification) involves third-party lab verification of biochar carbon content and stability.

First credit issuance typically lands **~2 months after installation** goes live.

Do you have existing operations we can reference?

Yes. We operate **Nigeria's first industrial biochar plant**, continuously processing palm kernel shells since December 2024. Happy to share site references and connect you with the operating team once we're in serious conversation.

What do you need from us — land, utilities, permits?

Three things:

- Your feedstock supply (1,000–5,000+ tonnes/year)
- Approximately **10m × 10m of covered space** near your processing line
- Access to **3-phase power** (~5–7 kW)

Permits, compliance, machine operation, and carbon credit certification — all handled by BIG.

How long from signing to first revenue?

Roughly **6 months from signed agreement to live operations**, then ~2 months until the first carbon credits are issued and sold. So plan on ~8 months from handshake to first payment.
