## Why it matters

Every product we process, every dehydrator we deploy, and every farmer we partner with creates measurable, lasting change in Nigeria’s food system.

🌍

#### Reduce Post-Harvest Loss

Up to 40% of produce in Nigeria never reaches consumers. Our processing and preservation technology dramatically changes that equation.

💰

#### Increase Farmer Income

By processing produce into shelf-stable products, farmers earn more per harvest and access higher-value markets year-round.

👩‍💼

#### Create Rural Livelihoods

We prioritise women and youth employment in our processing and distribution operations, building economic resilience in rural communities.

🥗

#### Nutritious Food Year-Round

Shelf-stable processing means Nigerians access nutritious food products long after the harvest season ends, improving food security.
