The Growth Lab programme of the Investment in Digital and Creative Enterprises (iDICE) Startup Bridge initiative has officially commenced, welcoming eight carefully selected Nigerian startups into a structured growth journey designed to strengthen their businesses, deepen their investment readiness, and position them for their next phase of growth.

This is the second arm of the iDICE Startup Bridge (the first being the Founders Lab), an initiative of the Federal Government of Nigeria, implemented by the Bank of Industry (BOI) and co-financed by the African Development Bank (AfDB), Agence Française de Développement (AFD), and the Islamic Development Bank (IsDB), with the aim of  supporting entrepreneurs at different stages of the startup journey, providing opportunities for capacity development, access to industry expertise, and pathways to financing.

Applicants underwent a rigorous, multi-stage selection process involving assessment, evaluation, and due diligence exercises to identify startups with the potential, readiness, and capacity to benefit from the programme. The process was designed to ensure that the final eight represent startups with a clear growth ambition and the potential to contribute meaningfully to Nigeria’s digital and creative economy.

With the selection process concluded, the commencement of Growth Lab marks another significant milestone for iDICE Startup Bridge, moving from identifying high-potential businesses to actively supporting their growth .  Through Growth Lab, each participating startup will access $100,000 (approx. ₦134million) in equity investment, alongside structured growth support, access to industry experts, investment readiness support and preparation for future opportunities.

The selected startups for this cohort of Growth Lab are: GoDash (Last-mile Logistics, Northwest), Telios (Energy, Southeast), Vantrace (Regtech, Southwest), KrosAI (AI, South-South), Leaflet (Agritech, Southeast), Lenol (Mobility, South-South), 360gadgetsafrica (Ecommerce, North Central), and SavvyBee (Fintech, Southwest).

As part of the programme launch, the founders of the selected startups recently participated in a series of in-person onboarding sessions, held in Lagos, which brought them together with key stakeholders, peers, and ecosystem enablers, providing an opportunity to build connections, gain insights into the support available to them, and develop a clear understanding of the programme’s expectations, structure, and growth objectives.

Commenting on the launch of Growth Lab, the National Coordinator of the iDICE Programme, Ife Adebayo, said:

“ _Each of these founders has earned their place through a rigorous and transparent process that tested not just their ideas, but how they run their businesses. Growth Lab is the Federal Government’s commitment to backing that discipline with real capital and real support. Through the Bank of Industry and our development partners, we are investing in these companies because we believe they can create jobs, attract further investment, and show what Nigeria’s digital and creative economy is capable of. Beyond funding, we will stand with them on the institutional and regulatory side, so that the next stage of their growth is not held back by barriers we can help remove.”_

Over the coming weeks, the eight startups will participate in programme activities focused on strengthening their business models, improving operational capacity, navigating growth challenges, and preparing for future investment opportunities.

## **About iDICE**

iDICE is the **Investment in Digital and Creative Enterprises Programme**, a **Federal Government of Nigeria intervention** designed to promote innovation, entrepreneurship, and job creation within the digital and creative sectors. The iDICE Programme is **co-financed by the African Development Bank (AfDB), Agence Française de Développement (AFD), the Islamic Development Bank (IsDB)**, and the **Bank of Industry (which also serves as the implementing agency).**

The iDICE Startup Bridge operates through two distinct entrepreneurship programmes: Founders Lab, an Incubator for entrepreneurs at the ideation phase, and Growth Lab – an Accelerator for post-MVP ventures ready to scale and raise institutional capital.For additional information and interview requests, contact [info@startupbridge.ng](mailto:info@startupbridge.ng)
