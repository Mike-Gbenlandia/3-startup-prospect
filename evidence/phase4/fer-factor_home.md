## [Tossdown](https://ferfactor.com/games/tossdown/)

You are a package courier with no brakes. Run, jump and dash through a city coming apart around you. Simple controls, tight movement, and just enough mayhem to keep you going one more round.

## Play these  right now

Six smaller games, all live. Four run in your browser and two are on your phone.

![Gundotz key art](https://ferfactor.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/images/gundotz/Gundotz+Logo.jpg) Screens & trailer

### [Gundotz](https://ferfactor.com/games/gundotz/)

Twin-stick shooter

One rule: movement is everything. Clean visuals, fast enemies, and a rhythm that clicks the moment you stop thinking.

[Play on itch.io](https://theferfactor.itch.io/gundotz)

![ChoreMaster key art](https://ferfactor.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/images/choremaster/Thumbnail.jpeg) Screens & trailer

### [ChoreMaster](https://ferfactor.com/games/choremaster/)

Cozy time attack

Grab a vacuum and go. Every level is a mess, a timer and a score to beat. Cozy until you start optimising.

[Play in browser](https://www.viverse.com/CxQ3GYs)

![Kickshot key art](https://ferfactor.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/images/kickshot/Capsule+Final.jpeg) Screens & trailer

### [Kickshot](https://ferfactor.com/games/kickshot/)

On-rails shooter

The path is fixed. The kills are yours. Kick in doors, clear rooms and chain takedowns for big scores.

[Play in browser](https://www.viverse.com/c5nRiSV)

![Null Fall key art](https://ferfactor.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/videos/nullfall/null+fall+banner.png) Screens & trailer

### [Null Fall](https://ferfactor.com/games/nullfall/)

Number puzzle

Drag glowing orbs onto the board and pair each number with its opposite. +3 meets −3? Zero point! The bigger the numbers, the bigger the blast.

[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noviac.nullfall) [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823532738)

![Crate Crashers key art](https://ferfactor.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/images/cratecrashers/CrateCrashers_FeatureGraphic_1024x500.png) Screens & trailer

### [Crate Crashers](https://ferfactor.com/games/cratecrashers/)

Roguelite dodgeball

Catch, throw and deflect the ball to smash your opponent's crates. A roguelite dodgeball brawler inspired by Knockout City and Rounds.

[Play on itch.io](https://theferfactor.itch.io/cratecrashers)

## A global studio.  Played everywhere.

We are an independent studio with players, collaborators and community all over the world. We care about one thing above the rest: how a game feels in your hands in the first five seconds.

So we start with movement and controls, then build everything else around them. Short to learn. Deep enough that the best players keep finding more.
