# Africa is not just playing games. We’re making them.

Since 2012, Maliyo Games has been on a mission to build world-class games, develop exceptional talent, and help create a thriving games industry in Africa. What started as an ambitious idea in Lagos has grown into a studio working across game development, global partnerships, education, research, and industry development.

Philosophy

## Build games. Build people. Build the industry.

1. 01

### Build Games


Commercially successful products that stand beside the best in the world, made in Africa and played everywhere.

2. 02

### Build People


Exceptional talent grows through training, mentorship and real production experience, so great ideas have the people to carry them.

3. 03

### Build the Industry


Africa’s gaming industry grows through access to markets, meaningful partnerships and a community committed to growing together.

Capabilities

## What we do.

1. 01

### Original Games & IP


We create and own original mobile games and characters rooted in African culture, built to grow into lasting franchises.

2. 02

### Game Development & Co-production


We design, build and ship games for global brands and studios, from concept through live operations.

3. 03

### Publishing & Distribution


Through strategic partnerships like [Guardian Games (opens in a new tab)](https://games.guardian.ng/), we help developers bring their games to market, reach new audiences, and unlock global opportunities.

4. 04

### Talent Development


Through GameUp Academy we train the next generation of African developers, artists and designers for real careers in gaming.

5. 05

### Industry Research & Partnerships


We publish research on Africa’s games market and work with partners, investors and institutions to grow the industry.


AI Principles

## Human Creativity. Powered by AI.

We’re using AI to unlock new possibilities for how people create, play, and learn. At Maliyo Games, we’re exploring how artificial intelligence can make gaming more personal, interactive, and accessible. From enabling players to bring their own content into our games and create unique experiences, to building an AI-powered learning companion that provides personalised guidance and support to learners on GameUp Academy, we’re putting powerful new tools in people’s hands.

Our approach is simple:AI expands what’s possible. People bring the imagination.

### Your Content. Your Game.

We’re bringing player creativity into our games, using AI to transform personal content into unique in-game experiences that make every player’s journey their own.

[Discover Kewa’s Fashion](https://www.maliyo.com/games/kewas-boutique)

### Your Learning. Your Pace.

Through GameUp Academy, we’re building an AI career companion that understands learners’ skills, identifies knowledge gaps, and provides personalised guidance to help them achieve their career goals.

[Learn more (opens in a new tab)](https://gameupacademy.com/)
