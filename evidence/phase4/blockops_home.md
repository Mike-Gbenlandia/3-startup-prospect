Blockops takes the security of your blockchain operations very seriously. We employ state-of-the-art encryption, secure node communication protocols, and regular security audits. Additionally, our infrastructure is designed to be resilient against common cyber threats, ensuring the integrity and confidentiality of your data


What support does Blockops offer for setting up and managing nodes?

Blockops offers comprehensive support for setting up and managing your blockchain nodes. This includes technical assistance, detailed documentation, and customer service support for troubleshooting. We aim to make the process as seamless as possible, regardless of your technical expertise.


What happens if I exceed my plan's usage limit?

If you exceed your plan’s usage limit, Blockops will notify you with the options to upgrade your plan or purchase additional resources per your needs. Your services will continue to run, but additional charges may apply if you choose to continue with over-usage without upgrading your plan.


How does Blockops handle data privacy and compliance?

Blockops is committed to data privacy and compliance with all relevant regulations, including GDPR. We implement strict data handling policies, ensure data encryption in transit and at rest, and conduct regular compliance audits.
