## Built from necessity. Grown by community.

Backel started with a personal problem: chronic digestive issues that no amount of conventional advice seemed to fix. Our founder, based in Lagos, began experimenting with gut-friendly recipes biscuits without common allergens, fermented drinks with live cultures, chocolates that nourish instead of harm.

What began as a kitchen experiment became a mission. Today, Backel is a women-led food company producing functional snacks and beverages from locally sourced Nigerian ingredients. Every product is made in Lagos with care, intention, and a deep belief that food should heal.

We're not here to preach. We're here to prove that healthy food can taste incredible and that your gut deserves better than what's currently on the shelf.
