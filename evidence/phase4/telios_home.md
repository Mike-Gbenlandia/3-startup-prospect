The problem

## Capital is ready.  Assets are ready.

Investors want African infrastructure, and developers have working sites that need funding to grow. The data that would connect them doesn’t exist yet.

Global capital$520B+in impact capital looking for African deals.

Distributed assetsThousandsof mini-grids that need standard data to scale.

An investor asks whether a site really produces what the developer says, how it compares with similar sites and whether a year of metered data exists; the developer can only send an unverified monthly spreadsheet.

### They can’t find  each other.

Information asymmetry keeps capital and assets apart. Three things are missing.

- A common format

Every inverter, meter and battery brand reports its data differently.

- Verified performance

Results are self-reported in spreadsheets, not metered at the source.

- Fast due diligence

Each deal takes months of manual checks before money can move.
