# Who we are

Learn2Earn was founded by Iyinoluwa Aboyeji, Kesiah Valkin and a team that has helped train and support thousands of engineers around the world.

![Learn2Earn Team](https://learn2earn.ng/images/new/campus-group-pix.png)

## Learn2Earn is not just another tech program — it's arevolution\|.

We are building a clear pathway into the AI economy for African talent.

At a time when AI is reshaping who gets to participate in the future, we believe Africans should not just consume technology, but build it. Learn2Earn is designed to train and deploy world-class builders who can compete globally and create real value.

The programme is structured around trust and shared responsibility. We invest in your training upfront, and once you secure paid work, you pay it forward by helping fund the next generation of learners.

This is not just education. It is preparation for ownership — of skills, of opportunity, and of the future.

## Our Mission

Our mission, clear from inception, is to forge a path where talented Africans over the next 10 years, can acquire high-demand, AI-focused skills without the burden of debt, earn while they train, and seamlessly transition into global tech roles.

Transcending traditional coding, we are rewriting the rules of opportunity, and establishing Africa as the most competitive source of tech talent globally, demonstrating our conviction that the next monumental AI breakthroughs will emerge from the most unexpected and vibrant communities, driven by a new generation of AI-native engineers.
