## Frequently asked questions

[See all FAQs](https://myvendy.com/faqs)

What does Vendy do exactly?

Vendy provides agentic payments infrastructure for social platforms. We help businesses accept payments directly within messaging apps, social media, and AI chatbots, making commerce conversational and seamless.

Does my customer need to download any app to pay me?

No. Your customers do not need to download any additional app. Vendy works natively inside the social platforms and messaging apps they already use, making checkout frictionless and instant.

Which plan is right for my growing Business?

We offer flexible plans tailored to businesses at every stage. Whether you're a startup processing your first transactions or an enterprise scaling across markets, our team will help you find the right fit. Start with a free account and upgrade as you grow.

What payment methods are supported via Vendy?

Vendy supports multiple payment methods including bank transfers, card payments, USSD, mobile money, and more. One integration gives you access to all payment channels across Africa and beyond.

Is Vendy only available in Africa?

Vendy is currently focused on African markets, with strong support for Nigeria and other key markets on the continent. We are actively expanding to more regions — reach out to our team to discuss availability in your market.
