ABOUT CRAFT PLANET


## TRANSFORMING PLASTIC WASTE   INTO SUSTAINABLE SOLUTIONS

![image](https://craftplanet.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/separator.png)

Craft Planet is a climate technology social enterprise addressing plastic waste pollution through innovative recycling and circular economy solutions. We recover and process plastic waste and transform it into sustainable building materials, while creating green jobs and opportunities for communities.

Our approach connects environmental action with economic and social impact, turning discarded plastic into valuable resources and supporting more sustainable development. Through technology, community engagement, and strategic partnerships, we are building practical solutions for a cleaner and more circular future.

## OUR MISSION & VISION

Craft Planet’s mission is to reduce plastic waste pollution by collecting, processing, and transforming plastic waste into sustainable building materials while creating opportunities for communities.

Our vision is a future where plastic waste is treated as a valuable resource, communities are empowered through green opportunities, and sustainable solutions contribute to a cleaner and more resilient society.

## FROM PLASTIC WASTE TO SUSTAINABLE BUILDING MATERIALS

Craft Planet was founded to tackle plastic waste pollution while creating practical solutions for sustainable development. We combine waste recovery, innovative processing, and sustainable materials to give plastic waste a new purpose.

Our work connects environmental action with economic opportunity—creating pathways for green jobs, community participation, and more sustainable construction.
