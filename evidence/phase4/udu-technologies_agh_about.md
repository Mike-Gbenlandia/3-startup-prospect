# Building Africa's AI Infrastructure

Africa GPU Hub is a GPU-as-a-Service platform on a mission to democratize high-performance computing across Africa. We provide researchers, startups, and enterprises with accessible, affordable GPU infrastructure to accelerate AI innovation on the continent.

## Our Mission

Make world-class AI computing accessible to every African innovator — from university researchers in Lagos to fintech startups in Nairobi, from healthcare AI teams in Cape Town to agritech builders across the continent.

## What We Offer

### GPU Cloud

Rent powerful NVIDIA GPUs by the hour, day, or month from data centers across Africa and India.

### AI Marketplace

Deploy pre-trained models optimized for African use cases and languages.

### Hardware Market

Purchase development kits, workstations, and GPU hardware with local support.

### Partner Network

Connect with solution partners and consultants across the continent.
