## Our Story:

## Pioneering Renewable Cooling in West  Africa

Founded on December 5, 2022, Rekool Access Technologies was born out of a critical need—affordable, reliable, and sustainable cooling solutions for healthcare,

agriculture, and  small businesses in Africa.

With over 40% of food wasted post-harvest due to inadequate cold storage and millions of vaccines lost due to unreliable refrigeration, we saw a pressing challenge that demanded innovation. Our mission is to redefine cold chain logistics using renewable energy-powered cooling solutions that bridge the gap between technology and accessibility

![](https://rekool.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/BAM0177-1024x684.jpg)

## Our Vision

To become West Africa’s leading renewable cooling company, ensuring safe food storage, reliable vaccine preservation, and sustainable business growth through innovative cold chain solutions.

## Our Technology

At Rekool Access Technologies, we design and build solar-powered, battery-integrated cold storage systems that guarantee continuous refrigeration even in off-grid and low-power environments. Our Vacci-Safe system is engineered with phase change materials (PCM) for thermal storage, AI-powered energy optimization, and IoT-enabled monitoring, ensuring maximum efficiency and reliability
