## The Journey: From Potential to AI Engineer

Learn by building, not memorizing. From day one, you'll work on real-world projects using today's most in-demand technologies and AI tools.

Our driven learning model is immersive, collaborative, and designed to help you grow.

[Play the Game](https://auth.learn2earn.ng/register)

1. ### Selection Game (90 minutes)



Play our free online game. It tests memory, and problem-solving; not prior knowledge. Do well, and you're invited to the Trials.



Remote. Can be done anywhere

2. ### Trials (5 days)



A high-intensity, in-person bootcamp. You'll learn to code, collaborate, and be pushed harder than you've been pushed before. Only the most determined move on.



On-campus. Full-time commitment required.

3. ### The Training Program (1 Year)



Build full-stack applications with AI at the core, create a strong, real-world portfolio, and graduate ready to step into a job.



On-campus. Full-time.

4. ### AI Specialization & Internships (1 Year)



Apply your skills in a real work environment. You'll go deep on AI while gaining hands-on industry experience — the bridge between training and your global career.



On-campus. Full-time.

5. ### Graduate and Get to Work



Step into 1 year of access to paid work through Talent Nation global talent agency.



You've made it.

## Frequently asked questions

Find everything you need to know to get started on your earning journey

Do I need to know AI before joining?

No. We build AI engineers from scratch. If you have the logic and the grit, we provide the AI-native environment to help you master the craft.

Why the focus on AI?

AI is the most significant technological shift since the internet. To be competitive in the global job market, you must be more than a coder; you must be an AI-native engineer.

We would get you ready for that future starting today.

How do I join Learn2Earn?

Start by playing our free, online selection game — it tests your logic, memory, and problem-solving skills.

If you pass, you'll be invited to join the Trials — a 5-day, in-person coding bootcamp that challenges your grit, collaboration, and learning ability.

Only top performers from the Trials are admitted into the full Learn2Earn program. Think you've got what it takes? [Play the Game](https://auth.learn2earn.ng/register)

Is there an enrolment or application fee?

No — there are no fees at all. The selection game, the training programme, and the job placement are completely free to you.

Instead of paying tuition upfront, you repay the cost of your free education by joining our global talent agency after graduation, where you'll work on exciting international tech projects for a minimum period of 1 year.

It's your chance to gain real-world experience, earn a salary, and give back

How does the selection process work?

It starts with our online selection game — designed to test your logic, memory, and problem-solving skills.

If you perform well, you'll be invited to the Trials — a 5-day, in-person coding bootcamp held at one of our campuses.

The Trials are intense, collaborative, and immersive. You don't need any tech background — just grit, curiosity, and the ability to learn fast. You'll work on real coding challenges in an environment that mimics real-world problem solving.

Only the top performers from the Trials are selected to join the full Learn2Earn program. Think of it as your audition for a life-changing opportunity.

Ready to take the first step? [Start the Game](https://learn2earn.ng/#)

How long does it take to complete the game?

The game takes about 90 minutes to complete. Most players finish within a few hours to a couple of days, depending on how much time they spend on it.

Top performers often complete it in 1-2 hours, but you're free to take breaks and play at your own pace.

What does Learn2Earn provide during training?

During the training phase, Learn2Earn provides:

- Full-time technical training on a world-class learning platform
- Access to learning infrastructure and campus facilities
- A desktop or laptop computer for programme use

Do you own the computer you receive?

No, not by default.

You are expected to care for the device and ensure it's in good condition at the end of the programme.

In some cases, you may be offered the option to retain the device by paying it down in installments over up to 24 months.

Is the programme full-time?

Yes.

Learn2Earn is an intensive, full-time programme. You are expected to:

- Show up every day
- Be punctual, focused, and engaged
- Treat the programme like a professional commitment

This level of commitment is essential to achieving the outcomes we promise.

What happens if you decide to leave during training?

You are free to leave at any time during training if you decide the programme is not right for you.

If you leave before certification, you are not required to pay anything. Your place will be offered to another learner who wants the opportunity.

What happens after I complete training and get certified?

Once you are trained and certified:

- Real, employable value has been created.
- At this point, you are expected to pay the opportunity forward so the next learner can be trained.

This happens primarily through paid work.
