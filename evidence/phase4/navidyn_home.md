Core Technology

## One intelligence layer, _built to scale_.

NeuroCore is Navidyn's developing mission software layer for perception, control integration, telemetry, and inspection data workflows. It starts with Omí and is designed to scale toward future robotic systems.

01

### Control Integration

Interfaces with vehicle control systems to enable stable, human-supervised underwater operation.

02

### Mission Data Workflow

Organizes video, sensor data, and inspection notes into structured outputs for faster engineering decisions.

03

### Perception & AI Assistance

Uses AI and computer vision to detect anomalies, recognize objects, analyze inspection video, and assist with inspection reporting.

04

### Autonomy Platform

Designed as a reusable software layer for increasingly intelligent and autonomous Navidyn subsea systems.

Engineering Philosophy

## Designed for _real underwater_ engineering constraints.

Omí is being developed from practical subsea requirements upward: stable maneuvering, sealed electronics, reliable power, usable inspection video, and maintainable hardware.

Every design iteration is focused on one goal: turning a working prototype into a field-ready inspection platform for African marine environments.

- 01Modular frame and payload layout
- 02Sealed electronics and pressure-rated housing design
- 03Vectored propulsion for stable maneuvering
- 04Camera and lighting system for inspection workflows
- 05Designed for future sensor and payload expansion
