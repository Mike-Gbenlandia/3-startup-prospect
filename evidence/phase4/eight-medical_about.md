About Us

Our Story

Our story is one woven with passion, Innovation and commitment to transforming emergency care response in Nigeria and Africa at large. Our Journey was birthed from a place of pain and loss of loved ones which fuels our vision to make access to care easier. We are an end to end platform that identifies and connects hospital, personnel, care, information and credit to users in 10 mins or less, we ensure accessibility, Improved patient care, and streamlined processes to care. Our team consists of health care personnel, product enthusiasts, who are continually building the operating system for accessing care. Join us in our mission to empower the African health care system.
