## SwitchFAQ

### Are these for Switch?

No. Joy-Con 2 controllers are not backward compatible with the original Nintendo Switch. shellify builds these for Switch 2.

### Do they work with Switch 2?

Yes, shellify offers custom Joy-Con builds compatible with Switch 2, with the same focus on color, fit, and finish.

### Can I customize shells and buttons?

Yep. You can build left, right, or pair Joy-Cons with custom shell colors, custom button colors, and D-Pad options on supported models.
