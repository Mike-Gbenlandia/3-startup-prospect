Our story

## Since 2012. Still Building. Still Playing.

1. 2012–2019



### A Bold Beginning



Founded in Lagos with a vision to build world-class games inspired by African stories.

2. 2020-2023



### Investing in Talent



Launched GameUp Academy to develop Africa’s next generation of game creators.

3. 2024–2025



### Going Global



Partnered with Disney on Iwájú: Rising Chef, expanding our creative reach and global partnerships.

4. Now



2026–Present



### Scaling Our Impact



Accelerating growth across games, talent, distribution, and industry development.
