## Emerging-market compliance should not be rebuilt from scratch.

Each regulated market has its own AML rules, fraud patterns, reporting formats, data residency expectations, and regulator workflows. Whether a local institution is operating in Nigeria or a global fintech is entering Kenya, the compliance foundation should already exist.

Local rules

Change by market

Thresholds, filing formats, escalation workflows, and regulator expectations differ across jurisdictions.

Fraud patterns

Move faster

Mule networks, account takeover, structuring, and synthetic activity evolve differently across markets.

Manual operations

Do not scale

Case queues, report drafting, and audit evidence become bottlenecks as transaction volume grows.

Legacy systems

Duplicate builds

Teams entering a market are often forced to rebuild the same AML, fraud, case, and reporting capabilities from zero.

## One intelligence layer. _Multiple regulatory markets._
