## The end of term, before and after

The same three weeks, run two different ways.

### The old way

- #### Weeks of compilation



Teachers rushing to calculate scores manually while you wait for the master broadsheet.

- #### Error-prone records



Missing scripts, calculation errors, and grade disputes that damage parent trust.

- #### Compromised exams



Malpractice and students sharing answers during assessments.


### With ParaLearn

- #### Instant compilation



Scores move from teachers' devices to the master broadsheet in real time.

- #### Verified accuracy



Automated calculations eliminate human error. What is recorded is exactly what is reported.

- #### Integrity first



Secure Computer-Based Testing flags tab-switching, so grades are earned, not shared.


## Why schools switch

Outcomes, not feature lists.

### Eliminate the end-of-term bottleneck

Broadsheets compile themselves as scores come in. Approval and publishing happen in clicks, whether you manage 50 students or 5,000.

### Assessments you can trust

Exams are monitored for malpractice. Interruptions and tab-switching are flagged, so the grades students get are the grades they earned.

### Give teachers their weekends back

Objective questions grade themselves and theory scores enter in seconds. Teachers spend less time calculating and more time teaching.

### Instant transparency

Once you approve a report card, students see it immediately on their secure portal. No lost papers, no confusion.

### A register that never goes missing

Attendance is marked in seconds, with one tap for a full class. Every record is stored by class and date, visible to administrators instantly.

### Lesson notes drafted in seconds

SabiNote AI generates structured, NERDC curriculum-aligned lesson notes on demand. Teachers walk into class prepared.
