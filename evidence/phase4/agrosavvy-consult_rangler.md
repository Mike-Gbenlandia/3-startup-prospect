# Agrosavvy Consult wins NGN 5 M in non-dilutive funding through NiYA-Cascador programme

Announced 16 Nov 2026 · Grant

This grant for Agrosavvy Consult, a company in Nigeria, was announced on 16 November 2026. It was worth NGN 5M, about $3.3K at that year's average rate. Cascador Entrepreneurial & Leadership Initiative Ltd/Gte, Federal Ministry of Youth Development and Nigerian Youth Academy took part.

Cascador Entrepreneurial & Leadership Initiative Ltd/Gte is an accelerator and investment initiative set up in Nigeria in 2019 as a company limited by guarantee. It targets mid-stage African businesses with proven market traction: at least one year of trading and either $50,000 in yearly revenue or $250,000 in capital already raised. It began as an education, advice and mentorship programme bridging local early-stage incubators and larger global accelerators.
