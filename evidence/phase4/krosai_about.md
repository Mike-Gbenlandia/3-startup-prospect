# The phone layer for voice AI in emerging markets.

We saw global CPaaS underserve the regions where voice still matters most. So we built the alternative.

[Get Started](https://cockpit.krosai.com/)

## Our story

KrosAI was started inside KrosWeb, a software studio building products for Africa and the broader emerging-market world. We kept hitting the same wall: every voice AI project we touched was bottlenecked on the same thing — telecom. Numbers took weeks to provision. Calls routed through Frankfurt and back. Local accents confused models trained on US English. So we built the layer we wished existed: a CPaaS purpose-built for AI voice, in the regions that mattered to us.

## What we're building

KrosAI is the phone-number-and-routing layer underneath your voice AI. We provision local lines in seven countries, stream audio bidirectionally with sub-20ms latency, and stay out of your AI stack. You bring the agent; we bring the telecom.

#### Emerging markets first

We build for Lagos, Nairobi, Jakarta and São Paulo before we build for San Francisco. The needs are different, and they deserve infrastructure designed for them.

#### AI-native, not AI-bolted-on

Every routing decision, every codec choice, every PoP placement is made with voice AI workloads in mind — not legacy IVRs.

#### Operator-friendly

Transparent pricing, no contracts, no procurement gauntlet. If you can sign up for Stripe, you can sign up for KrosAI.
