The studio

## Our story starts  with a big idea.

Founded in 2013 by Edu Shola, Deluxe began as a one-person studio with a vision: to build games and visual content that represent African culture, characters and stories.

Today, we’re a team creating original intellectual property with room to grow across platforms. Game development, animation and visual storytelling come together under one roof.
