## ABOUT TRYCTOR

Tryctor is a tractor and agricultural equipment manufacturer headquartered in Nigeria, dedicated to enhancing the productivity of smallholder farmers across Africa.

Our flagship product, the Tryctor 300, is renowned for its simplicity and effectiveness, making farming more accessible and efficient across the continent.
