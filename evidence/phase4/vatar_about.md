## Vatar raises $500K angel round

**Lagos, 9 October 2026.** Vatar, the company behind Lagos Life, has raised a $500K angel round from Abdulhamid "AB" Hassan, co-founder and CEO of Mono, Nathan Nwachuku, co-founder and CEO of Terra Industries, and Olugbenga "GB" Agboola, co-founder and CEO of Flutterwave.

Lagos Life puts a living, playable Lagos in the browser. More than 4.5M people have played so far, and more than 1.5M play on an average day.

The funding goes into the platform and into bringing new cities online.
