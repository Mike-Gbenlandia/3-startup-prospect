## About UpSmart Energies

UpSmart Energies is a Nigerian clean-energy social enterprise that provides renewable lighting and energy solutions to individuals, small businesses, refugees and communities, using local manufacturing (company LinkedIn description). Its website describes solar energy solutions in Nigeria. The company is headquartered in Jos, Plateau State, per its LinkedIn company page and RoundOS. Its CEO and founder is Moses Nweke. UpSmart Energies was one of nine NGN 1 million non-dilutive growth awards from the NiYA x Cascador Founders Programme, announced 10 November 2026.
