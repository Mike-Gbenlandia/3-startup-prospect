## Questions  Advertisers Ask

What is Bravoo for Brands?+

Bravoo for Brands is a platform that lets you launch interactive missions and promotions to an active, engaged community, turning attention into real participation and action.

What are Missions?+

Missions are tasks or campaigns you set up for Bravoo Citizens to complete—trying your product, sharing feedback, following your brand, and more—that reward participation.

Can promotions be included in my campaign?+

Yes. You can pair missions with promotions to boost visibility and put your brand, product, or offer in front of an active community.

Can I measure campaign performance?+

Yes. Bravoo provides real-time metrics on engagement, participation, and growth so you can track exactly how your campaign is performing.

What can I use Bravoo Campaigns for?+

Brand awareness, product feedback, community building, driving sales, or any goal that benefits from real audience participation.

What campaign goals can Bravoo support?+

From getting discovered, to building a community, to driving sales—Bravoo missions can be tailored to the specific goal your brand is working toward.
