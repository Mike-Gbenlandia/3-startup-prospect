Why Craft Planet


## Turning Plastic Waste into Affordable Housing in Africa

![image](https://craftplanet.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/separator.png)

Craft Planet is a climate technology social enterprise dedicated to tackling plastic pollution through innovative recycling solutions. We recover plastic waste and transform it into sustainable construction materials while creating green jobs, empowering communities, and advancing Africa’s transition to a circular economy.
