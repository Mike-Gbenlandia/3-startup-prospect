About Vendy

# Building the future of social commerce

We're on a mission to make payments as natural as conversations. Vendy powers the infrastructure that lets businesses sell anywhere people communicate.


## Our Mission

To democratize commerce by embedding payments into every conversation, making it possible for any business to sell where their customers already are.


## Our Vision

A world where every social interaction is an opportunity for commerce, and every business has access to the tools to make it happen seamlessly.


## Our Values

### Innovation First

We push boundaries and challenge conventions to build payment solutions that feel like a chat.

### Customer Obsession

Every decision starts with the question: how does this make our customers more successful?

### Trust & Security

We treat every transaction as sacred. Bank-grade security is not optional, it is foundational.

### Inclusive Growth

We believe commerce should be accessible to every business, regardless of size or location.
