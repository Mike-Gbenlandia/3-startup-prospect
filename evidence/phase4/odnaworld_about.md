# We Are Odnaworld

A woman-led agritech and food processing company on a mission to transform Nigeria’s agricultural value chain from the ground up.

Our Story

## Built on a Simple Question: Why Are Farmers Still Losing Half Their Harvest?

Odnaworld was founded with a clear-eyed understanding of Nigeria’s agricultural paradox: a country with some of the world’s most fertile land, yet rampant food insecurity and farmer poverty. The answer wasn’t growing more — it was losing less.

What began as an online grocery and fresh produce delivery service evolved into something far more strategic: a vertically integrated agritech and food processing company operating at the intersection of farmer technology, industrial processing, and modern ecommerce.

Today, Odnaworld operates a processing facility in Jos, a distribution network reaching Lagos and beyond, and a mobile dehydration technology programme that is quietly changing what’s possible for smallholder farmers across Nigeria.

Our Journey

## From Online Grocery to Agritech Company

﻿

2019

#### Founded as Online Grocery

Odnaworld launches as a fresh produce delivery service connecting urban consumers with Nigerian farms, operating from Lagos.

2020

#### Processing Pivot

Observing massive post-harvest waste among supplier farmers, Odnaworld begins investing in food processing and dehydration technology.

2024

#### Jos Facility Opens

Processing operations move to Jos, Plateau State — Nigeria’s food bowl — giving Odnaworld direct access to Nigeria’s best produce.

In Process

#### Mobile Dehydrators Launched

First mobile dehydration units deployed to partner farmers, piloting the cooperative ownership model and farm-level preservation technology.

2019–Present

#### Scale & Impact

50+ dehydrators deployed, B2B wholesale programme launched, 500+ farmers in the network, 200+ women and youth directly employed.
