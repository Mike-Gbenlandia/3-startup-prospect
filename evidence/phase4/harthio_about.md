# Proactive Mental Wellness That Travels With You

Start therapy in London, continue in Accra: your clinical progress travels with you. Harthio combines peer support, portable professional therapy, and 24/7 AI guidance to build emotional resilience wherever life takes you. Whether you're navigating anxiety, depression, addiction recovery, grief, or personal growth, we provide proactive care with continuity, empowering you to thrive, not just survive.

## The Story Behind Harthio

In 2023, our founder Oluwaseyi Akinlolu was going through a difficult health crisis that deeply affected his mental well-being. Despite being surrounded by people, he felt completely alone in his struggle. Traditional therapy was expensive and had long waiting lists, support groups felt impersonal, and friends, while caring, couldn't relate to his specific challenges or understand the depth of his experience.

That's when he had a simple but powerful realization: **somewhere out there was someone who had walked his exact path and would understand completely—not just intellectually, but emotionally.** The problem wasn't a lack of empathetic people—it was the lack of a way to find them when you need them most.

Harthio was born from this insight. We're not just another social platform or networking app. We're a bridge between people who need mental health support and those who can provide it, based on shared experiences, genuine understanding, and the healing power of being truly seen and heard.

## Your Complete Anonymous Mental Health App

Harthio is more than just another emotional support app. It's a comprehensive private mental health app designed for people who need someone to talk to anonymously without judgment. Whether you're looking to book certified therapists near you for professional counseling, connect with peers for shared experiences, or access our AI mental health companion for 24/7 support, Harthio provides multiple pathways to mental wellness. Our platform helps you find therapists by location across Africa, engage in peer support sessions, and utilize stress management app tools, all in one place.

### Book Certified Therapists Based on Location and Your Needs

Finding the right therapist shouldn't be complicated. Harthio connects you with licensed, vetted therapists across Africa based on your location, specific mental health needs, and personal preferences. Whether you need help with anxiety, depression, trauma, or relationship issues, our therapist near me matching system finds professionals who specialize in your challenges. Book online therapy sessions or in-person appointments at partnered therapy centers near you. Not satisfied? Switch therapists anytime. Your wellness data transfers seamlessly across our vetted network with your consent, ensuring continuity of care without starting over. This is professional mental health consultation made accessible, affordable, and flexible for African communities.

### Peer Support for Shared Experiences

Sometimes you need to talk to someone who has walked your exact path. Harthio's peer support connects you with people who truly understand your struggles, whether that's addiction recovery, grief, burnout, or life transitions. These aren't random strangers; they're carefully matched connections based on shared experiences and topics. While peer support is invaluable for feeling understood, we recognize that professional therapy provides clinical expertise that peers cannot replace. That's why Harthio offers both: book sessions with certified therapists for structured treatment, and connect with peers for relatable, judgment-free conversation. Your healing journey deserves both professional guidance and human connection.

### AI Mental Health Companion Available 24/7

Our AI mental health companion serves as your personal mental wellness tracker, providing evidence-based support whenever you need it. This isn't just a chatbot. It's a sophisticated anonymous therapy app alternative that offers overthinking help through proven CBT techniques. As a dedicated CBT app, it guides you through cognitive behavioral exercises, helps you challenge negative thought patterns, and provides immediate coping strategies during difficult moments. When you need someone to talk to anonymously at 3am, our AI companion is there, offering the kind of support that combines professional therapeutic approaches with genuine understanding. It's like having a therapist in your pocket, without the wait times or high costs.

### Advanced Mood Tracker & Recovery Tools

Mental wellness requires consistency and self-awareness. That's why Harthio includes a powerful mood tracker that helps you understand your emotional patterns through daily mood check-ins. Our platform goes beyond basic tracking. Whether you need a sobriety tracker for addiction recovery, a quit smoking app to monitor your progress, or a comprehensive addiction recovery app that supports multiple goals, we've got you covered. The sobriety tracker features milestone celebrations (7 days, 30 days, 90 days, 1 year), urge logging, and relapse prevention app tools that have helped thousands maintain their recovery journey. As a behavioral change app, we help you identify triggers, track progress, and build healthier habits one day at a time. Our mental wellness tracker transforms abstract feelings into concrete data, empowering you to see progress even when it doesn't feel tangible.

### Private, Anonymous Support Without Judgment

Privacy isn't just a feature. It's our foundation. As a private mental health app and anonymous mental health app, Harthio ensures all your conversations, mood tracking data, and recovery milestones remain completely confidential. You can talk openly about burnout recovery, emotional challenges, or addiction struggles without fear of exposure. Our emotional support app connects you with real people who've walked similar paths, offering peer support that feels authentic because it is. Whether you're experiencing burnout recovery from work stress, managing chronic anxiety, or navigating addiction recovery, you'll find others who genuinely understand. This emotional recovery app creates a judgment-free zone where vulnerability is strength, and asking for help is celebrated. Combined with our stress management app features, you have everything needed to reclaim your mental wellness on your terms, completely anonymously.

### Seamless Care Across Providers with Your Consent

Your mental health journey doesn't stop when you switch therapists or therapy locations. With your explicit consent, Harthio enables your wellness data (session notes, mood tracking history, recovery milestones, and treatment progress) to transfer securely across our network of vetted therapy partners. Moving to a new city? Your new therapist can access your history (with your permission) so you don't lose momentum. This continuity of care, combined with our affordable therapy options and location-based therapist matching, makes mental healthcare truly accessible across Africa. Whether you choose professional therapy, peer support, AI companionship, or all three, your progress is tracked, protected, and always in your control.

Harthio integrates every tool you need into one seamless platform. From the AI mental health companion that provides instant support, to the mood tracker that reveals patterns in your emotional life, to the sobriety tracker that celebrates your recovery wins, everything works together. Our relapse prevention app features help you identify warning signs before they become crises. The daily mood check-in creates accountability and awareness. The addiction recovery app tools provide structure when you need it most. And through it all, you have access to someone to talk to anonymously, whether that's our AI companion or a peer who's been exactly where you are. This isn't just another app you'll download and forget. It's a behavioral change app that meets you where you are and helps you get where you want to be.
