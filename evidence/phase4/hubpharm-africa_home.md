## Online Pharmacy _FAQs_

Everything you need to know about our medicines, delivery and pharmacy care.

**Still have questions?**

Is HubPharm Africa a licensed and trusted pharmacy?

Yes. HubPharm Pharmaceutical Limited (RC 1812043) is licensed by the Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (LAG20247B39C9) and is NDPC audit compliant (NDPC/AUDIT/24430). We were founded by two licensed pharmacists and have delivered over 400,000 medicine packs across Nigeria and 12+ countries.

Will my medicines cost more with HubPharm?

No. Your cost stays the same or lower. Our team works to find you the best price for authentic medicines, and we have saved patients more than N25 million on their medications.

How quickly will my medicine be delivered?

Same day or next day within Lagos, and within 48 hours nationwide. International delivery to West Africa, the UK, Europe, Australia and Canada typically takes 3 to 7 business days.

What medicines can I order?

Prescription and over-the-counter medicines, brand and generic, plus specialty therapies for cancer, rare diseases, fertility, diabetes, HIV/PrEP, sickle cell and more. Prescription-only medicines require a valid prescription, which our pharmacists verify.

Can I speak with a pharmacist, nurse or doctor?

Yes. Our care team includes pharmacists, nurses, doctors and other health professionals. Afiya is available 24/7 on WhatsApp and connects you to a licensed clinician during opening hours for consultations, medication advice and ongoing care.

Can my doctor send my prescription directly to HubPharm?

Yes. Doctors can send prescriptions directly to HubPharm Africa. We coordinate with your physician on any clinical details and make sure you get your medicines promptly. [Information for doctors](https://hubpharmafrica.com/for-doctors/).

Are your health screening tools free?

Yes. All our screening tools are free and private, and most take under 3 minutes. They support, but do not replace, a diagnosis from a clinician. [See all screening tools](https://hubpharmafrica.com/hubpharm-screening-tools/).
