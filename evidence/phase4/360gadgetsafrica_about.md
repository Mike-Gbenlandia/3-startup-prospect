# Our Story.

360GadgetsAfrica brings the proven circular tech marketplace model to Africa. We partner directly with vetted tier-1 distributors, corporate liquidators, and certified refurbishers, giving you access to premium devices from trusted marketplace sellers.

Every UK-Used devices listed undergoes a rigorous 50-point diagnostic check, backed by our platform warranty. and an accredited network of independent repair partners.

![360Gadgets Certified Circular Tech](https://terra03.s3.amazonaws.com/images/hero.webp)

## The 360 Standard   Tested. Warrantied. Clean.

Buying tech shouldn't feel like a gamble. We replace the uncertainty of informal open markets with strict quality charters, vetted distributor pipelines, and transparent warranties.

### 50-Point Inspection

Every UK-Used device is tested before listing. We verify screen response, battery health, camera focus, cellular radios, logic board integrity, and authentic component status.

### Vetted Distributor Supply

No unvetted open-market stalls. We partner exclusively with certified distributors, enterprise liquidators, and authorized refurbishers who pass our strict quality charter.

### Accredited Repair Network

We vet, audit, and partner with certified independent repair labs and technicians, holding them to strict OEM parts standards backed by our platform warranty.
