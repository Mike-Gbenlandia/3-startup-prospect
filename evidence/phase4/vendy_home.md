## Frequently asked questions

What is Kleevar?

Kleevar is an escrow-protected marketplace. Buyers pay into escrow, sellers list items (services are coming soon), and delivery is handled by a fellow member or by standard delivery, with payment released only once the buyer confirms the deal.

How does escrow protect me?

When you order, your payment is held by Kleevar instead of going straight to the seller. It is only released once you confirm your item arrived as described, so your money is never at risk while a deal is in progress.

Who delivers my order?

You choose. Have a fellow Kleevar member deliver, they offer a fee and you agree it, or use standard delivery. Either way you can track your order, and the delivery fee stays in escrow until it’s completed.

Can I return an item or claim warranty?

It depends on the seller. Many listings include a return policy or a warranty, shown on the product and saved to your order, when they do, a protection window after delivery lets you return the item for a refund or open a warranty claim. Escrow protection and disputes cover every order regardless.

What if something goes wrong with a deal?

You can raise a dispute. Kleevar reviews the evidence from both the buyer and the seller and makes a decision. Your funds stay locked in escrow until the dispute is resolved.

How do I earn as a delivery agent?

Register as an agent, set the areas you cover, then browse open delivery jobs and submit your fee. Once a buyer accepts, complete the pickup and delivery and your fee is paid to your wallet, minus a small platform commission.

How do I pay?

Pay for each order by debit or credit card, or by bank transfer, at checkout. Your payment is held in escrow and only released to the seller once you confirm the deal.

Are there any fees?

Kleevar charges a small platform fee on completed transactions, and delivery agents keep their fee minus a small commission. The exact amounts are always shown before you confirm anything.
