# Aeon extends pre-seed funding to $2 million to build sovereign cyber defence infrastructure for Africa and the Global South

Precious Ngwube

![aeon-team](https://images.ctfassets.net/iun3ergefu5h/5QC7LVBq937hMRiir04nZi/89cd7b13acceff6b00b0588bf066cfc7/Group_Shot_1__1_.png?w=1600&q=80)

Aeon extends pre-seed funding to $2 million to build sovereign cyber defence infrastructure for Africa and the Global South

Today, we are pleased to announce that Aeon has extended its pre-seed funding round to $2 million, with new investments from Launch Africa Ventures and Octerra Capital.

This follows our initial $1 million pre-seed round announced in September, led by Terra Industries, with participation from Resilience17, the investment firm of Flutterwave co-founder and CEO Olugbenga “GB” Agboola, alongside other investors.

This investment brings us another step closer to achieving our mission of building the cyber defence prime for the Global South and developing the technology, talent and capabilities required to protect the digital infrastructure our economies depend on.

## Building for an evolving cybersecurity landscape

Over the past decade, Africa and the Global South have made significant progress in building digital infrastructure. From financial services and telecommunications to healthcare, energy and government services, digital technology has become fundamental to how our economies operate.

However, the need to secure this infrastructure is becoming increasingly urgent.

Artificial intelligence is fundamentally changing the economics of cybercrime. Capabilities that previously required considerable technical expertise, time and resources are becoming increasingly accessible, enabling attackers to operate at greater speed, scale and sophistication.

At the same time, many organisations continue to rely on fragmented security tools that are expensive to operate, difficult to integrate and require significant specialist expertise.

“AI is fundamentally changing the threat landscape. The technical barriers that previously limited sophisticated cyberattacks are falling, and the speed and scale of these threats are increasing,” said [**Samuel Ogbonyomi**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samuel-ogbonyomi-31a578171/) **, Co-founder and CEO of Aeon.**

“Our response cannot simply be to adopt more tools. We need to build the infrastructure, technical expertise, and capabilities to defend the systems our economies depend on. We believe Africa should be actively building that future.”

Our experience building and managing cloud infrastructure for African businesses through PipeOps gave us firsthand insight into these challenges. It also strengthened our conviction that there is an opportunity to build globally competitive cybersecurity technology from within the region, informed by the realities of the organisations it is designed to protect.

That conviction is at the heart of Aeon.

“For two years we have looked for a team building cyber defence for Africa's critical infrastructure from the ground up,” said [**Uwem Uwemakpan**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/uwemuwemakpan/) **, Head of Investments at** [**Launch Africa Ventures**](https://www.launchafrica.vc/) **.** “Aeon's founders spent years running cloud infrastructure for African businesses at PipeOps, and they built this product around the gaps they saw there. As AI makes attacks cheaper to launch and harder to detect, banks, grid operators and telecoms across the continent need security built for their own systems and regulators. That is what Aeon is building.”

## Building the technology to defend our digital infrastructure

At Aeon, we are building an integrated cyber defence ecosystem that gives organisations greater visibility, control and protection across their digital environments.

Our approach brings together two core products.

- **Aeon Console**, our AI-powered cybersecurity and compliance platform, enables organisations to identify, prioritise and remediate vulnerabilities across their applications, cloud infrastructure and endpoints.

- **Aeon Edge**, our network security device, provides organisations with greater visibility and control over connected devices, network activity and potential threats.


Together, these products are designed to address the fragmentation in security operations, helping organisations move more effectively from identifying risks to taking corrective action.

Our focus is on protecting organisations across critical sectors, including financial services, energy, healthcare, telecommunications, government and defence, where the consequences of cybersecurity incidents extend beyond individual organisations to the economies and communities they serve.

## Our commitment to digital sovereignty

For us, digital sovereignty extends beyond where data is stored. It is about having the technology, expertise and operational capabilities to understand, manage, secure and defend the infrastructure on which our societies depend.

“As cybersecurity threats evolve and the complexities surrounding data sovereignty and protection intensify, AEON's approach to integrating fragmented security operations is particularly compelling. For Octerra, AEON is a natural extension of our conviction and investments in the trust layer of the digital economy,” said [**Ashim Egunjobi**](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ashim-egunjobi/) **, Managing Partner at** [**Octerra Capital**](https://octerracapital.com/) **.**

We believe the Global South should play an active role in building the cybersecurity technologies that will shape its digital future.

Our ambition is to contribute to a future where organisations have greater control over their digital assets, where countries have the capabilities to protect their critical infrastructure, and where individuals can participate in an increasingly digital society with greater confidence.

We are grateful to Launch Africa Ventures, Octerra Capital, Terra Industries, Resilience17 and all our investors for their conviction in our vision and their support as we continue to build.

The Global South has demonstrated its ability to build technologies that transform economies. We believe it can also build the technologies that defend them.

**That is the future we are building at Aeon.**
