# About Drych Digital Limited

IT'S ALL IN OUR NAME

TO SYNC CREATIVITY WITH SUSTAINABILITY.

## Art doesn't have to be boring because it is eco

Drych Digital is a pioneer Green IT startup and creative agency. We decarbonise digital platforms by measuring and reducing digital carbon emissions, and creating low-carbon websites, software, and platforms that load fast, and save mobile data and battery—even on 3G, 4G, and 5G.

- With our **Website Carbon Evaluator,** you can check how much carbon your website emits.
- With**Eco Sleep**, your platform stays light, fast, and energy-smart when idle.
- And with our **creative studio**, you get bold and expressive designs, animations, branding and creative/interactive experiences.

Wild Concept? Maybe

_We found a way to merge digital climate action with creativity_

We love what we do. We started because we love this.

Creating. Building. Mapping out businesses and bringing them to life.

That was the entire blueprint.

We call it Drych Digital.

**Drych** is Welsh for Mirror and Digital is, well, self-explanatory.

Put it together and you get Digital mirror, but we prefer Digital Reflection.

So we hold a mirror up to your business and show you what’s there.

And what could be.

You know those things you say when you look in the mirror?

Say it to us…exactly like that.

**_We can handle it_**
