# About SewPRO

We’re on a mission to revolutionize tailoring in Africa by equipping tailors with smart tools that bring order, freedom, and growth.

At SewPRO, we believe that every tailor deserves more than just a sewing machine — they deserve the tools, structure, and systems to build a thriving business. For decades, tailors and fashion entrepreneurs have struggled with the same challenges: Lost or mixed-up client measurements Missed delivery dates and constant pressure Difficulty keeping skilled workers Running businesses without the structure to grow We know this problem firsthand. Our founder, Andrew Ogbeh, started as a young tailor, sewing to pay his school fees from secondary school through university. He experienced the stress of juggling clients, deadlines, and the lack of tools to run tailoring like a real business. That journey inspired the creation of SewPRO — a platform built to solve the problems tailors face every day.

![Our Story](https://www.sewpro.app/assets/images/MockUps_5.png)

## Our Story

SewPro was born when we realized tailors were losing precious time and income managing clients with notebooks and scraps of paper. We built SewPro to make tailoring smarter — from storing measurements to sending reminders and managing payments.

## Our Mission

To empower tailors and fashion entrepreneurs with smart digital tools that bring structure, efficiency, and growth to their businesses.

## Our Vision

To be the leading fashion-tech platform in Africa, transforming tailoring from a hustle into a scalable, profitable business that sustains generations.
