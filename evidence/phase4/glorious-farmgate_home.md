## About Glorious Farmgate

Glorious Farmgate is at the forefront of agritech innovation in Nigeria, transforming poultry farming with our solar‑powered automation solutions. By streamlining feeding processes, we help farmers lower labor costs, curtail waste, and enhance flock health.

Founded by a team of agricultural specialists and technology visionaries, we blend hands‑on farming expertise with state‑of‑the‑art automation to deliver robust, field‑tested systems that thrive in real‑world conditions.
