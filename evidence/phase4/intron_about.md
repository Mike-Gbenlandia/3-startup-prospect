# About Us

INTRON

## unexplained, unexplored, and undervalued DNA/RNA segments

Intron is the infrastructure layer for voice interactions across Africa, with industry leading speech-to-text and text-to-speech APIs to power several context-specific, domain-specific, accented, and local language voice applications like clinical ambient listening, medical dictation, judicial and legislative transcription, voice commands for mobile app interactions, reproductive health voice bots, call center inbound and outbound automation, meeting minutes, voice authentication, and so much more.
