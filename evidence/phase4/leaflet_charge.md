# iDICE backs eight startups with $100,000 each: who they are

- [No comments](https://charge.africa/idice-growth-lab-startups/#respond)

![iDICE Growth Lab: Bank of Industry office in Gombe](https://charge.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/idice-startups-funded.jpg) A Bank of Industry office in Gombe. The bank implements iDICE. Photo: Yahuzaishat / Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0), cropped

##### Author

##### [Claudia](https://charge.africa/author/claudia/)

Eight Nigerian startups, from North-West logistics to South-South AI, are each getting $100,000 (about ₦134 million) in equity investment from iDICE, the federal government’s digital and creative enterprise programme. They make up the first iDICE Growth Lab cohort, part of the iDICE Startup Bridge, Daily Trust reported on 19 November ( [Daily Trust via allAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202611200061.html)).

> **Key facts**
>
> - **The money:** $100,000 per startup as an equity investment, not a grant. When it opened applications in July, iDICE said the entry cheque buys 7.5% of each company, with up to $250,000 more in follow-on investment for startups that hit growth conditions ( [ThisDay](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/07/21/idice-opens-applications-for-growth-lab-to-support-high-potential-nigerian-startups/)).
> - **Who pays:** iDICE is a federal government programme run by the Bank of Industry and co-financed by the African Development Bank, Agence Française de Développement and the Islamic Development Bank.
> - **The iDICE Growth Lab cohort:** GoDash, Telios, Vantrace, KrosAI, Leaflet, Lenol, 360gadgetsafrica and SavvyBee.
> - **What’s next:** the iDICE Startup Summit moves to the Ilorin Innovation Hub on 23 November, with the national final in Abuja on 30 November ( [ThisDay](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/11/19/founders-tasked-on-structure-compliance-at-lagos-idice-summit/)).

Why it matters: the cohort is spread across five of Nigeria’s six geopolitical zones (none of the eight is from the North-East), and two of the eight are from the South-South. For founders outside Lagos, that is a sign that a government-backed equity cheque doesn’t depend on being in Yaba.

## Who are the eight iDICE Growth Lab startups?

Daily Trust lists each company by sector and region:

- **GoDash:** last-mile logistics, North-West.
- **Telios:** energy, South-East.
- **Vantrace:** regulatory technology, South-West.
- **KrosAI:** artificial intelligence, South-South.
- **Leaflet:** agritech, South-East.
- **Lenol:** mobility, South-South.
- **360gadgetsafrica:** e-commerce, North-Central.
- **SavvyBee:** fintech, South-West.

The founders went through assessments, evaluations and due diligence before selection, and were onboarded in person in Lagos. “Each of these founders has earned their place through a rigorous and transparent process,” said Ife Adebayo, iDICE’s national coordinator. “Beyond funding, we will stand with them on the institutional and regulatory side.”

## What do the startups get?

Besides the $100,000, the Growth Lab offers business development help, access to industry experts and support to get ready for outside investment. iDICE described it in July as a 12-week accelerator for tech-enabled startups that have already built a minimum viable product.

When it opened applications, iDICE said it would pick 12 startups. Eight have been announced so far; the programme hasn’t said whether more will follow.

## What the iDICE Growth Lab terms mean for founders

The terms put a price on each company. Selling 7.5% for $100,000 implies a value of about $1.33 million after the investment. The follow-on money, up to $250,000, comes only if a startup meets growth conditions set by iDICE.

Government money is still equity. Before taking a cheque like this, founders should read the shareholder agreement closely and know what rights the investor gets.

## How does the rest of iDICE work?

iDICE, short for the Investment in Digital and Creative Enterprises Programme, backs young Nigerians building in tech and the creative industries. The iDICE Growth Lab is the second stage of its Startup Bridge, after the Founders Lab for earlier-stage founders. The Startup Summit tour runs alongside it: the southern preliminary was held in Victoria Island, Lagos, on 18 November, with Lagos commissioner for innovation, science and technology Olatunbosun Alake among the speakers.

## How can I apply to the next iDICE cohort?

iDICE hasn’t announced dates for the next Growth Lab or Founders Lab. Watch the Bank of Industry and iDICE channels, and apply only through official government or BOI pages: never pay anyone to submit an application for you.

## What’s next

The Ilorin preliminary is on 23 November, and the national final takes place at Bolton White Event Center, Abuja, on 30 November.

## Quick answers

### Is the iDICE $100,000 a grant?

No. It is an equity investment. When it opened applications in July, iDICE said the $100,000 entry cheque buys 7.5% of each company.

### How much equity does iDICE take?

7.5% for $100,000, which implies a value of about $1.33 million after the investment. Startups that meet iDICE’s growth conditions can get up to $250,000 more in follow-on investment.

### Which startups are in the iDICE Growth Lab?

GoDash, Telios, Vantrace, KrosAI, Leaflet, Lenol, 360gadgetsafrica and SavvyBee.

### Which parts of Nigeria are represented?

Five of the six geopolitical zones. None of the eight startups is from the North-East.

### Will iDICE pick more startups?

iDICE said in July it would pick 12. Eight have been announced so far, and the programme hasn’t said whether more will follow.

### How can I apply to the next iDICE cohort?

iDICE hasn’t announced dates. Apply only through official iDICE or Bank of Industry pages, and never pay anyone to submit an application for you.
