## **Mission Statement**

ColdHubs prevents post-harvest losses, improves the livelihoods of smallholder farmers and food supply chain actors by providing affordable, solar-powered cold storage and IcePoints facilities, refrigerated transportation and packaging, making safe and nutritious food available for local consumption.

## **Vision Statement**

Our vision is to empower smallholder farmers, food supply chain actors and rural communities through innovative, sustainable solutions that enhance food security, increase profitability, and promote environmental sustainability.

![](https://coldhubs.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/10th-Anniversary-Logo-2-800x800.jpg)
