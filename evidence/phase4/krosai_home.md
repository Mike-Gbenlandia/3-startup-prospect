## How It Works

Get your AI voice agent live on fully compliant phone numbers in minutes, not months.

### Provision Local Numbers

Instantly search and activate real local phone numbers compliantly across our live markets. KYC, compliance and fraud detection built-in.

KrosAI handles routing, audio streaming, fraud detection, and compliance under the hood — sub-second latency on real phone lines, in every market.
