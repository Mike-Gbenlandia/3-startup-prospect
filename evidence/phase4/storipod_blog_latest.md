# Chude Jideonwo Is Betting on Storipod: From 200,000 Users to 2 Million

The FourthMainland Fund is backing Storipod, and Chude joins us as Board Chair. Here is what has moved this year, including movies on Storipod, and the road to 2 million users.

![Storipod](https://blog.storipod.com/storipod-logo-dark.png)

James Nelson

Co-founder, Storipod

![Chude Jideonwo Is Betting on Storipod: From 200,000 Users to 2 Million](https://prodstoripodbucket.blob.core.windows.net/prod-storipod-assets/images/1795386832273-chude-jideonwo-storipod-cover.webp)

Dear friends and partners of Storipod,

This week, [Techpoint Africa reported](https://techpoint.africa/news/chude-jideonwo-invests-storipod/) that Chude Jideonwo is investing in Storipod through The FourthMainland Fund and joining us as Board Chair. Here is the story, what has moved since our H1 update, and where we are heading next.

> Storipod's long-term goal is to ensure that the next generation of creators have a place where they can monetise their content and IP, and access a distribution platform.

## Bootstrapped to 200,000 users. Backed by Chude Jideonwo.

Storipod grew from under 2,000 users to more than 200,000 in 18 months without raising venture capital. Chude's thesis is simple: Africa does not lack stories or talent, it lacks the technology and commercial infrastructure to distribute and monetise them. Global platforms keep failing African creators through sudden suspensions, takedowns and demonetisation, and Africa needs at least one major platform whose growth depends on creators earning more and owning their audiences.

Speaking to Techpoint, Chude said: "He has solved a hard problem with the limited resources."

Watch Chude explain why he is backing an app built in Lagos: [Why I am backing an app built in Lagos (#WithChude)](https://youtu.be/DBHBc1l1UrU). You can also read the [announcement](https://techpoint.africa/news/chude-jideonwo-invests-storipod/) and Techpoint's [exclusive feature](https://techpoint.africa/insight/chude-jideonwo-invests-in-storipod/).

## The highlights

- **200,000+ users**, with 150,000+ active creators earning on the platform on a 70/30 revenue split.
- [**Narrative Landscape Press**](https://techcabal.com/2026/04/13/storipod-clinches-rights-chimamanda-digital-push/) brought authors including Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie to readers in 170+ countries.
- **Smarter product:** a rebuilt recommendation engine, Storipod Voice AI narration, and Loop, our one-thumb vertical storytelling format, all lifting engagement.
- **Creators earn more:** Stori Views payouts are up 50%, with borderless payouts in 32+ countries via BASE by Coinbase and [Busha](https://busha.io/blog/storipod-partners-with-busha).
- **Our first paid brand campaign** delivered a 15.4% click-through rate against a 0.1% to 0.5% in-app norm.

## One more thing: Storipod now has movies

We started with stories you read. Then we gave them a voice. Then we put them in a Loop. Today, the platform that pays writers becomes a cinema in every pocket. Browse, watch the trailer, unlock a title for a small naira price, save it and share it. [Read the thinking behind it.](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/vacuum-amazon-cant-see-how-lagos-startup-plans-books-james-nelson--0cbie/)

## The road to 2 million users

Two million users is where our advertising model starts generating meaningful revenue. Here is what we are building to get there:

- **Storipod Tasks:** a marketplace where brands launch UGC campaigns and 150,000+ creators earn verified, automatic payouts.
- **Referral and affiliate engine** with UseAktivate: creators earn up to 30% commission on purchases from their communities.
- **Exclusive content:** originals such as the "Dear Diary" series, deeper publisher partnerships, and film distribution through Cinema.
- **Stickier sessions:** a wider Voice rollout and stronger recommendations at scale.
- **More African markets and brand partners.**

## About Storipod

Storipod is a social storytelling and entertainment platform built in Africa. With more than 200,000 users, the platform brings together user-generated stories, professionally published books, comics, audio and emerging entertainment formats in one discovery-driven experience.

Storipod provides creators and rights holders with infrastructure for digital distribution, content protection, discovery, monetisation and payments, with a focus on making African content more affordable and accessible.
