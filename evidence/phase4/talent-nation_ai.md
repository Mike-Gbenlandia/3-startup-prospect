## Whyworkwith _us_

Exceptional talent. Without the traditional constraints.

- ### Engineering quality


Our engineers are equipped to design, build and deploy production-ready AI systems. From systems architecture and integration to testing, evaluation and ongoing maintenance, we focus on the engineering discipline required to make AI work reliably in enterprise environments.

- ### Built to scale


We develop our own engineering talent through our academy Learn2Earn, with approximately 1,400 fellows currently in training. This gives us a growing pipeline of engineers to support enterprise demand.

- ### Better economics


Our scale lets us deliver serious AI engineering capability at a fraction of the cost of traditional consulting and talent models, with transparent scopes and pricing agreed upfront.

- ### Flexibility


Work with us on a defined project, engage a dedicated engineering team or recruit engineers directly. The model depends on your technical requirements, internal capabilities and preferred level of ownership.
