Why we exist

## Built from both sides of the deal.

We’ve been the creators chasing payment and the marketers chasing results. Both wanted the same thing: a simple, trusted way to work together and get paid. That’s what we’re building.

Our mission

### Make earning as a creator easy.

Finding paid work, agreeing a fair price and getting paid on time should take minutes, not weeks of DMs and chasing invoices. Everything we build removes a step between a creator and their pay.

Our vision

### Every creator, successful at the work they do.

Not just the famous few. We want the creator with 1,000 followers in Lagos, Nairobi, São Paulo or Manila to run creating like a real job, with real clients, fair pay and room to grow.

## What we believe

Creators get paid.

On time, every time. It's the whole point.

Small is mighty.

Nano and micro creators move people because people trust them.

Pay for what works.

No retainers or lock-in. Businesses pay per campaign or per sale.

Built for where creators are growing.

We started in Africa, where global creator tools didn’t fit. Now we’re taking that to Brazil and Southeast Asia: local currencies, local creators, local brands.
