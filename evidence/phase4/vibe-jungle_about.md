About

## AI is the camera, not the director.

Vibe Jungle is an AI entertainment company — not an AI movie company. We create original entertainment across film, music, images, podcasts, advertising and games, we develop our own artists and IP, and we make entertainment-led work for brands.

Everything comes out of the same room: the same directors, the same characters, the same sound department. Every frame is written, directed, scored and signed off by people.

**Six formats** Film, images, music, podcasts, advertising and games — one kitchen.

**One planet** Build the world once, then keep making things inside it.

**Human-led** Directed and scored by people who have spent careers doing it.
