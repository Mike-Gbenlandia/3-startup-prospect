## Questions, answered

The things people ask before connecting a provider.

### What is CostGraph?

A cloud cost platform that works from your actual bill. It ingests FOCUS billing data, reconciles it to your invoice, attributes every dollar to teams and workloads through virtual tagging, and puts GraphAI on top to find, explain, and fix waste.

### How does CostGraph integrate with existing tools?

Connect a provider’s billing export and CostGraph reads it in place, no agents on your machines. The operator adds Kubernetes detail, and the MCP server brings your cost data into Claude, Cursor, Codex and the other coding agents your team already uses.

### What benefits does CostGraph offer to teams?

Every team sees its own spend, on the same numbers finance sees. Rules tag resources once, past and future, so attribution stays right as things change, and rightsizing arrives with the savings attached for the owning team to approve.

### How does GraphAI enhance cost optimization?

Ask it a question in plain language and it answers from your bill, with the query behind every number. It spots what changed, explains why, and turns findings into reports and dashboards you can share.

### Is CostGraph suitable for multi-cloud environments?

Yes. AWS, Azure and Google Cloud land in the same FOCUS shape, alongside Kubernetes and AI provider spend, so you compare and attribute across all of them in one place.
