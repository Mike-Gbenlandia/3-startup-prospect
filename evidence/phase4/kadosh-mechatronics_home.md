## Homegrown capability, from idea to working product.

Kadosh Mechatronics is building **homegrown mechatronics and robotics capability**: smart systems, IoT solutions, prototypes, and machine-learning and AI-enabled systems.

We partner with tertiary institutions, startups and industry to create innovative solutions, taking ideas all the way to functional, tested products, and we train the engineers who will take that capability further.

08 — Questions

## Frequently asked.

Quick answers about Kadosh Mechatronics, our builds, training and store.

What does Kadosh Mechatronics do?

Kadosh Mechatronics is a Nigerian mechatronics and robotics company, based in Akure, that is pioneering homegrown mechatronics and robotics capability, building smart systems, IoT solutions, prototypes, and machine-learning and AI-enabled systems. It partners with tertiary institutions, startups and industry, as well as R&D centres, government, NGOs and schools, to create innovative solutions, taking ideas from concept to functional, tested products (machines, IoT devices, automation systems and lab test rigs, with the software that runs them), and provides training and facilitated workshops that grow technical talent.

Where is Kadosh Mechatronics located?

Our workshop is at the Kadosh Mechatronics Building, behind Chicken Republic, FUTA Junction, Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria. We work with clients across Nigeria and support builders elsewhere in Africa remotely.

Can you build a custom machine or prototype for my business or startup?

Yes. Through Kadosh Build you share your idea and our engineers return a scoped plan, parts list and timeline. You can build it yourself with our guidance, co-build with us, or have us deliver the complete, tested build.

Do you build lab equipment for universities and polytechnics?

Yes. Our builds include a forced-convection heat-transfer test rig, a smart static and dynamic testing machine and a multi-probe temperature monitoring and logging system. We also support final-year projects through the Kadosh Build Idea Hub.

Do you offer robotics and embedded systems training in Nigeria?

Yes. Kadosh Academy runs hands-on robotics, embedded systems, IoT, CAD/CAM, 3D printing, AI and software training in Akure and online, plus free step-by-step tutorials on Kadosh Build. Tech Kiddies offers robotics and coding classes for children.

Where can I buy Arduino, ESP32 boards and sensors in Nigeria?

The Kadosh Store sells development boards such as Arduino and ESP32, sensors, electronic components and Kadosh kits, priced in naira, with delivery across Nigeria and pickup in Akure.
