## About Zannytecture

Zannytecture is a Lagos-based upcycling and sustainability business founded by Zainab Kadiri-Lawal. It makes handcrafted furniture and décor from discarded tyres and PET bottles, sells upcycled products such as bags and side tables, and runs waste-to-wealth workshops, school sessions and community clean-ups. Its website reports more than 500 people trained. Zannytecture was one of nine NGN 1 million non-dilutive growth awards from the NiYA x Cascador Founders Programme, announced 10 November 2026.
