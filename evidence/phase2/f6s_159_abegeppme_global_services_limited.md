## About AbegEppMe Global Services Limited

AbegEppMe is a service-based e-commerce platform that connects artisans, handymen, workmen, licensed professionals, and Service providers with customers seeking their services through a user-friendly website and mobile application. AbegEppMe provides a seamless platform for users to search, book, and pay for services while ensuring quality assurance, trust, accountability and security like never before.
