**Nigerian startup PeoplePulse has developed an affordable, AI-powered workforce management platform designed to help African small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) manage their people and automate routine HR processes.**

Founded in June 2025, [PeoplePulse](https://www.peoplepulseafrica.com/) grew out of HR and business consultancy Bridgemead Consulting, which has worked with SMEs on recruitment, HR outsourcing, employee management and training.

The platform brings employee records, onboarding, leave and attendance management, payroll support, performance management, workforce analytics and compliance into one place.

“Many growing African businesses still manage their HR operations using spreadsheets, WhatsApp, paper records and disconnected tools. At the other end of the market, enterprise HR platforms can be too expensive or complicated for smaller organisations,” Adetola Abolarinwa, co-founder and chief product officer, told Disrupt Africa. “PeoplePulse is designed to bridge that gap: it is accessible enough for an SME but robust enough to support a growing workforce.”

The startup, which has an 11-strong team, competes with the likes of SeamlessHR and BambooHR, but believes its combination of affordability, simplicity, localisation and AI-powered automation sets it apart.

“We did not begin by making assumptions about what SMEs needed; we had already spent years working directly with them and understanding their operational problems,” Abolarinwa said.

Funded by its founders and a friends-and-family round, PeoplePulse piloted its platform with around 280 employees across eight companies between May and July 2026, with all eight converting into paying customers. It was also selected as one of the top 11 finalists for Cycle 7 of the Women in Tech Accelerator Nigeria.

The startup charges between NGN2,000 (US$1.5) and NGN3,500 (US$2.5) per employee per month across three subscription tiers.

“Our focus is not simply on acquiring registrations. We are concentrating on successful onboarding, active usage, retention and expanding adoption within each customer organisation,” Abolarinwa said.

PeoplePulse is focused on building a strong customer base in Nigeria, with Bridgemead’s network of more than 80 businesses providing a warm pipeline, before expanding into other African markets.

“We see PeoplePulse as a pan-African opportunity, but we intend to build a strong foundation in Nigeria before expanding aggressively,” said Abolarinwa.
