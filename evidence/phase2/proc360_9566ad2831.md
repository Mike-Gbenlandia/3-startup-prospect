## About this round

US$10,000 grant to each of the five winners of cycle 7 of the Standard Chartered Foundation Women in Tech Accelerator in Nigeria (total US$50,000); Fara Popoola, founder/CEO of Proc360, named a winner. Delivered with Village Capital and Enterprise Development Centre (EDC), Pan-Atlantic University (implementing partners, not linked as funders). date\_announced = date of the BusinessDay announcement post (2 Oct 2026); ceremony and disbursement dates not stated.
