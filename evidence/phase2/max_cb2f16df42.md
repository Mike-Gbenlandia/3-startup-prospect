## About this round

Series B3 extension. Nithio's financing was announced on 12 Oct 2026 (Nithio press release) and described as extending MAX's Series B3 funding round to a cumulative USD 29m. Nithio's contribution amount and instrument (equity vs debt) were not disclosed, so the amount is left unknown. The USD 29m is a cumulative round total which reporting says includes the USD 24m January 2026 equity-and-debt package (recorded on fundings 1506902/1506901) and BII's USD 5m second-close Series B3 investment (BII investment page; not separately recorded); it is not recorded here as new capital to avoid double counting. Closing date not disclosed.
