# Nigerian media entrepreneur Chude Jideonwo backs Storipod, becomes board chair

Nigerian media entrepreneur Chude Jideonwo has backed Storipod through the FourthMainland Fund and will serve as board chair as the platform expands its African creator economy business.

Nigerian media entrepreneur Chude Jideonwo has invested in Storipod, a mobile platform where African writers, publishers, and studios publish stories and earn from readers.

The funding comes from the Fourth Mainland Fund, a $500,000 fund established to invest in African creators. Jideonwo will also join Storipod as board chair.

FourthMainland is the group behind YNaija, Joy Inc., Chude Jideonwo Presents, and The Masterlist. Most of its businesses make content, and this will give it a stake and a board seat in a company built to distribute that content and collect payment for it.

The new capital will go into Storipod’s technology, its catalogue and its commercial partnerships. It will also go toward finding better ways for African intellectual property to reach and earn from audiences in different markets.

_“The FourthMainland Fund is investing in Storipod, and I am joining the company as board chair because the growth I see at the back and front end has crossed the point where my admiration should become participation,”_ [Jideonwo said](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-i-am-investing-200000-user-social-platform-future-chude-jideonwo-spxve/).

Storipod founder James Nelson said Jideonwo’s role gives the company a partner who understands both the creative work and the business systems required to scale it.

Storipod was founded by Nelson, who previously worked at Interswitch, Coca-Cola HBC, AXA, [Shuttlers](https://thecondia.com/shuttlers-secures-4-million-equity-funding/), and [Edukoya](https://thecondia.com/edukoya-shuts-down/). Creators on Storipod earn through paid stories, subscriptions, and tips and keep 70% to 80% of the revenue generated, depending on the product and tier.

The startup has been largely bootstrapped and now has over 200,000 users, over 12 million story reads, and a reach across more than 170 countries.

In April, Storipod partnered with Narrative Landscape Press, the Nigerian publisher behind Chimamanda Ngozi Adichie and other award-winning African authors.
