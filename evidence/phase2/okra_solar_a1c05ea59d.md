## About this round

British International Investment (BII) committed a $7 million debt facility (working capital for upfront procurement of solar panels, batteries and long-lead components) to Okra Solar, a mesh-grid provider active in Nigeria; announced 29 Oct 2026 per trade press (Launch Base Africa, Africa Private Equity News, Innovation Village). Interest rate, tenor and repayment terms not disclosed. Article describes BII as the sole financier; no valuation disclosed.
