Gsubz ( [gsubz.com](https://gsubz.com/)), the Nigerian digital services platform known for data, airtime and bill payments, has expanded into cross-border e-commerce. Through Gsubz Store, Nigerians can order goods directly from Chinese factories at wholesale prices, pay in naira, and collect their orders at Jumia pickup stations across the country.

The move marks a shift for a company that, since 2021, has built a base of more than 285,000 registered users on everyday digital services. Its Android app has passed 100,000 downloads with a 4.5-star rating on Google Play, and the app is also available on the Apple App Store.

## **A large market with small-business barriers**

China is Nigeria’s largest source of imports. National Bureau of Statistics data reported by Nairametrics shows that Nigeria imported ₦19.79 trillion worth of goods from China in 2025, its widest trade deficit with any single country.

For small importers and online sellers, access to that market has remained difficult. Paying Chinese suppliers usually means buying dollars, using international cards with spending limits, or relying on agents and middlemen. Delays in dollar payments can also cost importers money, because suppliers often hold prices for only a short time.

## **How Gsubz Store works**

A buyer searches for a product on the Gsubz website or app, sees the factory price in naira, and pays from their Gsubz wallet, which is funded by bank transfer. Gsubz handles the purchase from the supplier in China.

Buyers choose between air shipping (15 to 25 days) and sea shipping (70 to 90 days) for larger or heavier orders. Customs clearance is included, and there is no minimum weight or volume. Gsubz charges a 5% service fee on the value of the goods, with no fee on shipping or local delivery. Goods are delivered to the Jumia pickup station the customer chooses, so customers do not deal with ports or clearing agents.

A free [China to Nigeria shipping calculator](https://gsubz.com/tools/china-shipping-calculator.php) on the Gsubz website shows current air and sea rates and estimates costs for common items.

## **From airtime to imports**

Gsubz was founded in 2021 by Great Oputaolisa, a First Class Computer Science graduate of the University of Benin. The company is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission as Gsubz Enterprises Limited.

![image 7](https://techpoint.africa/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/image-7.jpeg.webp)

_“We started Gsubz to make data and airtime easier and more affordable for Nigerians, and that is still at the heart of what we do,” said Oputaolisa, founder and CEO of Gsubz. “But customers who run small businesses kept telling us that buying from China was too complicated, too risky and too dependent on dollars. We built Gsubz Store so a trader in any part of Nigeria can buy from a factory the same way they buy data on our app, in naira and in a few minutes.”_

## **Everyday services remain core**

Alongside the store, Gsubz continues to offer the services that built its user base:

- Data and airtime on MTN, Airtel, Glo and T2 (formerly 9mobile)
- Electricity tokens for prepaid meters on every DisCo
- DStv, GOtv and StarTimes subscriptions
- WAEC, NECO and JAMB PINs
- Recharge card printing for retailers
- International travel eSIMs paid in naira

These services are delivered automatically, 24 hours a day, through the Gsubz website and its apps on Google Play and the App Store. Gsubz also provides an API for developers and resellers.

[Gsubz Store](https://gsubz.com/store/) is available now on gsubz.com and in the Gsubz app for Android and iPhone.
