## About VestGas

VestGas is an AI-powered insurance and delivery platform that ensures households and businesses never run out of cooking gas. In Nigeria, over 13 million homes and SMEs face unreliable gas supply, long delivery delays, refill fraud, and unpredictable costs in a $3B market. Families are stranded mid-meal, restaurants lose customers, and businesses struggle with downtime because there is no reliable way to track, predict, or guarantee gas refills.
