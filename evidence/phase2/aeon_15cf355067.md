WHY IT MATTERS

Aeon’s case for the product is that cyber incidents across Africa have caused more than $3 billion in losses since 2019 while existing tools are fragmented and lack the intelligence to defend across multiple attack surfaces. Its $1 million round is intended to develop the products, expand the engineering and business-development effort, and turn pilots with financial-services and cloud-infrastructure businesses into commercial deployments, alongside a Terra joint venture combining physical and digital security for Global South contracts.
