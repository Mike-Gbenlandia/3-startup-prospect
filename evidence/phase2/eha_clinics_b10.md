# EHA Clinics Receives $1.25mllion Capital Reinvestment From EHA Impact Ventures

[EHA Clinics Communications](https://www.eha.ng/team/eha-clinics-communications)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65fd46fa9aaad26a950d79c3/6a70908e14b90d82ec6822c6_MTNATU%2087.jpg)

On July 28, EHA Clinics received a $1.25 million reinvestment from EHA Impact Ventures (EIV) at the Insights Learning Forum 2026 held in Abuja. This capital funding by EIV team was presented to EHA Clinics by Ramatu Abdullah, Senior Coordinator of Executive Programme Support at EHA Impact Ventures, and received by Dr. Ifunanya Ilodibe, CEO of EHA Clinics.

This new capital marks EIV's third investment in EHA Clinics in the past few years. The reinvestment aims to support job creation, improve healthcare access in underserved communities, and enhance antenatal care for women facing barriers to consistent prenatal support.

Evelyn Castle, CEO of EHA Impact Ventures, stated that the reinvestment reflects EIV’s commitment to “support businesses that demonstrate both operational resilience and measurable social impact." She added that “transformative healthcare solutions require long-term support, which is why we (EIV) are committed to backing organisations that are expanding access to quality care while building sustainable healthcare systems.”

In her presentation, Dr. Ifunanya Ilodibe, CEO of EHA Clinics, said that the funding "will enable EHA Clinics to expand access to quality healthcare, strengthen maternal and antenatal services, and continue reaching communities that have historically faced barriers to care."

EIV's continued investment is a strong vote of confidence in EHA Clinics' track record and commitment to quality care. Over the years, EHA Clinics has built a reputation for delivering comprehensive, compassionate primary healthcare. They combine innovative technology with a skilled medical workforce to serve the communities that need it the most.
