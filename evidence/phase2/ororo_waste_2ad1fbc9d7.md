## About this round

Up to NGN 1.4 billion (~$1M) via a tranched corporate note from Rising Tide Africa, the women-led pan-African angel network (122+ members in seven countries, 39 early-stage investments). First tranche disbursed 16 Sep 2026; further tranches possible through 16 Dec 2026; publicly reported 29 Sep 2026. Funds drums, trucks, hub storage, feedstock purchases and working capital (same-day mobile-money payment of ~5,000 informal collectors) and expansion from three collection hubs (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt) toward 40+ hubs and 150,000+ tonnes of ISCC-certified used cooking oil a year by 2031.
