## About this round

$4M blended financing from All On (through its Demand Aggregation for Renewable Energy Technology, DART, programme) and UK impact-investment platform Energise Africa (Ethex), with results-based financing support from Nigeria's Rural Electrification Agency tied to verified connections. Energise Africa's share includes about GBP 600,000 (~$760K) raised from UK retail investors via a Maskh bond. Funds 19 solar mini-grids across Jigawa and Bauchi states serving almost 100,000 people (households, businesses, public institutions and productive-use customers). Maskh already operates 14 sites in Bauchi with 3,000+ customers and 400+ kW installed. No equity/debt/grant split disclosed; no lead named.
