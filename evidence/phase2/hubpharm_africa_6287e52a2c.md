HubPharm Africa has raised $30K to expand its tech-enabled specialty pharmacy platform, which works to improve access to authentic medicines for patients with chronic and complex conditions across Africa and the diaspora.

The company, which launched in 2024, pairs a coordinated care team of pharmacists and health coaches with Afiya, an AI health companion that delivers medication reminders, adherence support, refill prediction, and health education through channels like WhatsApp. HubPharm says it has supported more than 10,000 patients and delivered over 500,000 medicine packs since launch, while improving medication adherence by 42%.
