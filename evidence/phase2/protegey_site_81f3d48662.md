THE INTELLIGENCE LAYER

## A fraud signal can mean more when it is connected to the right context.

Fraud rarely respects institutional or geographic boundaries. A pattern that appears isolated in one environment may become more meaningful when considered alongside other relevant signals. PROTEGEY is designed to help participating institutions identify recurring fraud patterns through permitted, non-biometric signals and authorized data-sharing arrangements. Depending on the integrations and permissions in place, relevant indicators may include device-risk signals, account patterns and transaction-related fraud signals.

WHAT THIS MEANS

- Better context for investigating suspicious activity.
- The ability to identify relevant recurring patterns across participating institutions.
- A more connected view of risk, subject to data availability, permissions and participating institutions' configurations.

FAQ

## Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to questions about Protegey's role and mission.

What is Protegey used for?

Protegey is used by financial institutions to detect and prevent fraud in real time. It enables teams to share anonymized fraud alerts and intelligence across our secure network to stop professional fraudsters before they attack your system.

Is Protegey a bank or a regulator?

No, Protegey is a technology platform. We provide the infrastructure that allows banks, fintechs, and regulators to collaborate. We do not process payments ourselves nor do we act as a legal oversight body.

How is Protegey different from internal fraud systems?

Internal systems only analyze your own data. Protegey provides a cross-institutional view, alerting you to threats that have been detected by other partners in the network, giving you a wider and more proactive defense.

Can multiple institutions share alerts?

Yes. Our Signal network specifically enables secure, privacy-preserving sharing of fraud signals between verified participants. This allows the entire ecosystem to benefit from collective intelligence.

Is Protegey focused on Africa?

Yes. Protegey is purpose-built for the unique challenges of the African financial landscape, with deep integration and coverage for regional systems like GIM-UEMOA and GIMAC.
