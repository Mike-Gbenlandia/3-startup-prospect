## About Budgexa

Budegxa is a money companion designed for young Africans who struggle to manage inconsistent income, track spending, and build savings.

It analyzes how users earn and spend in real time, provides personalized insights, and nudges better financial decisions. Unlike generic finance apps, it is built around local financial behavior such as irregular income, multiple accounts, cash usage, and informal saving patterns, making money management simpler, more relevant, and actionable.
