# Lodapoint launches truck-hailing platform with over 10,000 trucks engaged

The launch follows a B2B pilot involving real freight movements, which Lodapoint says helped it refine its technology and operating model before going to market.

Nigerian logistics technology company [**Lodapoint**](https://lodapoint.com/) has launched its truck-hailing platform, connecting businesses and individuals moving goods with vehicle owners and transport operators across Nigeria.

The company has engaged more than 10,000 trucks across different categories as it builds out its supply network.

The launch follows a B2B pilot involving real freight movements, which Lodapoint says helped it refine its technology and operating model before going to market.

“ _Our objective isn’t simply to put truck booking online. We’re building a marketplace where businesses can access transport capacity more efficiently and transporters can access more demand_,” said Ahmad Mustapha Ado, Founder and CEO of Lodapoint.

Lodapoint is a Nigerian mobility and logistics technology company building a single platform for moving people, packages, and goods.

All drivers on the platform undergo background checks and vehicle inspections. The app includes in-app emergency features, live location sharing, and 24/7 customer support.

Customers provide a pickup point and destination, select the vehicle type they need, and request it through the app. Available categories range from vans and pickups for smaller loads to larger trucks for heavier freight, covering both intrastate and interstate movements.

Customers can track their shipments in real time while goods are in transit.

The platform targets a logistics market that still relies heavily on phone calls, personal contacts, and manual negotiations to find trucks. According to the company, it is looking to aggregate that fragmented demand and supply digitally.

Farouk Umar Aminu, CTO of Lodapoint, added, “ _Different loads require different vehicles, routes and operating requirements. The technology has to account for that complexity while keeping the experience simple for the customer_.”

Truck hailing is the first stage of Lodapoint’s rollout. The company plans to launch an on-demand parcel and delivery service across Kano, Lagos, and Abuja, before expanding to other Nigerian cities.

Tricycle and ride-hailing services are expected to follow. Beyond Nigeria, Lodapoint has plans to expand into Kenya, Ghana, South Africa, Morocco, Egypt, and Uganda.
