## About this round

SCF #45 Build award to build a Stellar settlement rail letting users send USDC and receive local currency via M-PESA, Airtel Money and bank accounts in Africa (Kenya, Nigeria, Ghana, Uganda), using Stellar anchor standards and Circle CCTP. The SCF site shows SCF #45 in 'Notification & Award Distribution'; Minisend's own post is dated 30 Sep 2026 and the BitKE / News Crypto lists ran 1 Oct 2026, so 2026-09-30 is used as the announcement date. XLM-denominated award, USD value as stated. Company-reported traction: >$500,000 settled across 10,000+ orders for 1,000+ users.
