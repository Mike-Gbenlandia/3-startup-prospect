Fuelventa has raised $45K in pre-seed funding to build digital infrastructure for fuel commerce. The startup is targeting a fragmented but essential industry, connecting filling stations, depots, refineries, logistics operators, and inventory sponsors across the downstream energy value chain.

Its technology is aimed at bringing operational and financial visibility to the movement of every litre - from forecourt operations, shifts, sales, cash and inventory management to depot fulfilment, restocking, haulage, and FuelCycle funding. The company says the goal is a connected ecosystem where fuel, data, operations, and capital move more efficiently, helping businesses reduce operational friction and improve accountability.
