## About Crawdwall Capital

Crawdwall Capital builds the financial infrastructure layer for Africa’s event economy.

We solve three major problems:

Event organizers lack access to structured capital.

Investors avoid events due to high risk and poor governance.

There is no standardized execution or accountability system.

Our platform:

Vet organizers and events

Structure milestone-based investment deals

Hold funds in escrow with controlled disbursements
