## About ULN Together Technologies

ULN Together Technologies builds AI-powered beneficiary intelligence software for governments, NGOs, humanitarian organizations, and development partners. Through our flagship platform, UpliftNigeria, organizations can register, verify, manage, and monitor beneficiaries across social intervention programs using biometric verification, AI-powered fraud detection, automated workflows, and real-time analytics.
