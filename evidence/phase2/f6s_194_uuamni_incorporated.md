## About UUAMNI Incorporated

UUAMNI builds the training data and the sovereign GPU compute for AI in African languages, starting with Igbo.

The problem runs two directions. AI is measurably less safe outside English: refusal rates near 90% in English fall to 35 to 55% in Yoruba, Hausa and Igbo (LSR Benchmark).
