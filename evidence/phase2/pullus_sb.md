A new infusion of capital is set to boost local poultry production across Africa, as Pullus Africa announced it has raised $500K in pre-seed funding. The startup, which leverages data-driven technology to modernize the poultry supply chain, aims to empower farmers and enhance efficiency from farm to market.

Pullus Africa's platform is designed to address critical gaps in the continent's agricultural infrastructure. By helping poultry farmers facilitate access to guaranteed premium markets, quality inputs, extension services, and essential finance, the company is working to strengthen the entire supply chain and drive sustainable growth.
