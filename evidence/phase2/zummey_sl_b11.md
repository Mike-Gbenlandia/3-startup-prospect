## About Zummey Technologies

Zummey Technologies (trading as Zummey Logistics) runs an on-demand delivery platform in Benin City, Edo State. Customers order via the Zummey app or website, are matched with a rider within minutes and get real-time tracking from pickup to delivery, with fees starting from a few hundred naira; merchants can plug Zummey in as their delivery partner. The company uses a fleet of electric bicycles to cut emissions and, per its VC4A profile, also connects local farmers directly with consumers and supports women farmers with training and market information.
