## About FLEZPAY

FlezPay is an AI-powered Business OS and unified commerce infrastructure built for small and growing businesses in Africa and emerging markets.

FlezPay enables retailers to manage their physical and digital businesses from one synchronized platform, combining POS, inventory, payments, online storefronts, logistics, payroll, marketing, invoicing, tax compliance, and AI-powered business intelligence.
