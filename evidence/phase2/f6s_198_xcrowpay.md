## About Xcrowpay

Xcrowpay is building the Trust Layer for the Global South. I am solving the $700 million fraud problem that paralyzes digital commerce, specifically within the supply chains of emerging markets. My company provides an API first escrow infrastructure designed to enable "Market & Value Chain Transformation." We eliminate the Trust Tax, which is the economic loss caused when international buyers and local regenerative producers are too afraid to trade.
