## About Nahme

Most event photos are wasted.

Thousands of images get taken… but the people in them never find them.

Nah-me fixes that.

Nah-me is an AI-powered event photo platform that helps attendees instantly find every photo they appear in—without downloading any app or creating an account.

Each event gets a unique access code. Attendees simply enter the code, take a selfie or upload a photo, and our AI scans thousands of images in seconds to deliver their personalized gallery.
