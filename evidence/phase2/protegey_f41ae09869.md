##### PROTEGEY Secures Pre-Seed Funding To Expand Fraud Intelligence & Compliance In Africa

PROTEGEY, an Africa-focused fintech risk and fraud prevention startup, has secured pre-seed funding backed by LvlUp Ventures and angel investors as it prepares to expand its fraud intelligence and compliance infrastructure across the continent.

The company was founded by CEO Amadou Diallo and CTO Ndeme Yvan, who are building an AI-powered platform combining identity verification, fraud prevention, risk management and compliance tools.

PROTEGEY is also developing a shared fraud intelligence network that allows regulated financial institutions to identify threats across platforms and borders without sharing raw customer data.

The founders say the funding will accelerate product development and expansion, although they did not disclose the amount, valuation or specific markets targeted.
