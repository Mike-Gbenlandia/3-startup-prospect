## About Harthio

Harthio is a Nigerian mental health and wellbeing platform that describes itself as a proactive wellbeing platform helping people recover from difficult moments and maintain mental resilience. Company materials describe daily mental-baseline tracking (Wellness Score), access to therapy and discreet medication support. Headquartered in Akure, Ondo State, Nigeria per its LinkedIn company profile. Won the N20 million third-place prize in the Social Impact category at Wema Bank Hackaholics 7.0 (grand finale, Lagos, 6 November 2026).
