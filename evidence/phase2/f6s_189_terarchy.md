## About Terarchy

Terarchy is an invoice financing and verification platform helping African businesses unlock cash tied up in unpaid invoices. Many suppliers complete work for reputable buyers but wait 30, 60, or 90 days to get paid, leaving them without enough working capital to fund operations, pay workers, or take on new contracts.
