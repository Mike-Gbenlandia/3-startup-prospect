## About African Intelligence Cloud

African Intelligence Cloud (AIC) is a decision-intelligence platform that aggregates and analyses economic, governance, demographic, and risk data across all 54 African countries. Using AI/ML and LLMs, AIC converts fragmented open datasets into structured, queryable intelligence — enabling governments, development finance institutions, investors, and research institutions to make evidence-based decisions at continental scale.
