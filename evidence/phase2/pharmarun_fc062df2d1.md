## About Pharmarun

[![Screenshot of Pharmarun website](https://liners.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fassets.liners.com%2Fstorage%2Fv1%2Fobject%2Fpublic%2Fmedia%2Ftools%2Fpharmarun%2Fscreenshot.webp%3Fv%3D1773089584741&w=3840&q=75)](https://www.pharmarun.africa/?utm_source=liners.com)

Pharmarun is an on-demand online pharmacy platform for individuals in Nigeria who want to order medications, access clinical guidance, and receive home delivery.

Key capabilities include:

- **Medication ordering and delivery** through a network of **2,000+ pharmacies**, with **discrete doorstep delivery** and nationwide coverage (including delivery within **3 hours** in locations such as Lagos, Ogun, Port Harcourt, and Abuja, and **24 to 48 hours** elsewhere)
- **Medical consultations** with **licensed doctors and pharmacists**, plus **prescription verification** to support safer dispensing
- **Medication management tools** such as **automatic refills**, **medication reminders**, **interaction checkers**, and health utilities (for example, **BMI calculators** and health tips)
- **Flexible payments**, including a **buy now, pay later** option available via fintech partners

Available on **Web**, **Android**, and **iOS**.

It serves a **B2B2C** audience: consumers ordering medicines and consultations, plus **pharmacies, hospitals, and insurance providers** that integrate to support refills and medication adherence for patients.

Notable in Nigeria for combining a large pharmacy network with delivery logistics and in-app medication support, while also offering multiple ordering channels beyond the app and web (including phone, email, WhatsApp, and social media).

## Frequently Asked Questions About Pharmarun

### What is Pharmarun and how does it work?

Pharmarun is an on-demand medication delivery platform that aggregates multiple pharmacies and logistics partners to help you access medications quickly and conveniently. You can order medications through their website, and they deliver to your location within three hours in major Nigerian cities like Lagos, Ogun, Port Harcourt, and Abuja. The platform also offers automated refills for chronic conditions, free pharmacist consultations, and health tracking tools.

### How much does Pharmarun cost?

Medications are priced the same as in neighborhood pharmacies, with an additional delivery fee applied. Pharmarun also offers a Buy Now, Pay Later option through fintech partners, allowing you to pay in installments.

### What countries does Pharmarun operate in?

Pharmarun currently operates in Nigeria, serving 25 cities including Lagos, Ogun, Port Harcourt, and Abuja. The company has plans to expand to all 36 Nigerian states and the Federal Capital Territory, then extend to other African nations.

### How do I sign up for Pharmarun?

To sign up for Pharmarun, you can either download their mobile app or create an account on their website and start an order or subscription through the “Place Order” or “Create account” option.

### Does Pharmarun have a mobile app?

Yes, Pharmarun is available on iOS and Android. You can [download Pharmarun on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ng/app/pharmarun/id6499256702?utm_source=liners.com) for iPhone, or [get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=africa.pharmarun&utm_source=liners.com) for Android.

### Who is Pharmarun best suited for?

Pharmarun is suited for individuals managing chronic conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and asthma who benefit from automated refills, as well as businesses such as hospitals, insurance companies, and startups looking to reach their customers through their B2B2C model.

### How do I contact Pharmarun support?

To contact Pharmarun support, you can email experience@pharmarun.africa or call +234 913 289 1193, and they also provide a contact form on their “Contact Us” page.

### Does Pharmarun have a plan for teams or businesses?

Yes, Pharmarun offers B2B2C services for businesses including hospitals, insurance companies, and startups, helping them reach their consumers through the platform.

### What popular product is Pharmarun an African alternative to?

Pharmarun is an African-built alternative to [CVS Pharmacy](https://liners.com/african-alternatives/cvs-pharmacy) and [Walgreens](https://liners.com/african-alternatives/walgreens).
