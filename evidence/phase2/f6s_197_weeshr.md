## About Weeshr

Weeshr is a social commerce and gifting platform for emerging markets.

We enable people to easily discover, send, and deliver meaningful gifts—such as food, cakes, and essentials—while helping SMEs and online vendors grow revenue from high-intent, occasion-led purchases.
