## About this round

Google.org grant (reported as non-equity) to deploy AI tools through HeliumMum maternal health rollout across Lagos, Delta, Kano and Oyo states; reported as N3.4 billion (about $2.5 million). Helium Health was the only Nigerian organisation among 15 recipients of the $30M Google.org Impact Challenge: AI for Government Innovation.
