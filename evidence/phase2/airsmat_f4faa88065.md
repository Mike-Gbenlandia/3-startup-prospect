## About this round

$150,000 from Village Capital's Africa Ecosystem Catalysts Facility (a USD 4M pilot facility funded by FMO and the Netherlands Enterprise Agency, RVO), part of a combined $450,000 first Nigeria deployment shared with fintech Trade Lenda ($300,000). Deals were sourced by Africa Fintech Foundry. Instrument not disclosed. Funds will complete and commission AirSmat's commercial biochar-fertiliser production facility and increase output.
