01 — Our Narrative

## The Isolation Trap

Fraudsters thrive in the gaps between institutions. In a siloed digital economy, a single bad actor can exploit multiple platforms undetected because intelligence isn't shared. When data is fragmented, defense is reactive, and the ecosystem remains vulnerable.

Collective Intelligence

Protegey was built to break these silos. We've engineered Africa's first real-time, decentralized early-warning network. By turning individual signals into shared intelligence, a threat detected by one becomes an instant defense for all—creating a 'herd immunity' effect for the entire financial backbone.
