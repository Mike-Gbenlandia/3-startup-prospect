## About this round

SCF #44 Build award (Integration track, End-User Application) for 'Blink for Merchants': Bluetooth proximity crypto payments at physical checkouts with instant NGN settlement to merchants' bank accounts; ~252 users and 2,360+ transactions since its April 2026 launch. Tranches $15,000 / $22,500 / $30,000. Award listed in the SCF #44 round recap published 24 Jul 2026 (round closed for submissions 14 Jun 2026); Stellar Nigeria / Dealroom / Technext publicised the Nigerian winners on 19 Aug 2026. XLM-denominated award, USD value as stated by SCF.
