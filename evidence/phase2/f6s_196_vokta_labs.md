## About Vokta Labs

Vokta Labs builds African AI communication infrastructure, starting with VoxAfrica: a Nigerian English AI voiceover product for marketing videos, product ads, campaign videos, event promotions, launches, and social media promos. The current production layer uses Gemini-powered voice generation while Vokta Labs continues longer-term proprietary speech model development for African markets.
