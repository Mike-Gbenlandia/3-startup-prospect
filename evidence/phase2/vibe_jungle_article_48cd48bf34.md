[**Vibe Jungle Limited**](https://www.vibejungle.ai/) has raised $400k in a founder-led funding round. The Lagos-based company uses artificial intelligence alongside human production to offer music and video services to independent artists and filmmakers across Africa.

“ _It’s more than money. The investment brings us financial backing, technology, creative assets and intellectual property, everything we need to build faster and make more,” said the company._

Founded by [**Keziah Aboribo**](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/keziah-aboribo-03a5b21a2) in 2023, Vibe Jungle runs a hybrid model that combines AI-generated content with human-led production. Aboribo started the company after seeing the financial difficulties faced by young Nigerian musicians.

Many invested heavily in studio time and production, yet they found it hard to earn money from their music. This inspired her to create a platform that provides a more cost-effective entry into the industry for creators.

Its music arm works with songwriters and producers to create songs, some of which are sold to established artists.

External artists can license tracks from the company’s catalogue, adapt them with their own vocals and retain copyright over the new recording, while Vibe Jungle holds rights to the underlying composition.

_“Because the entertainment industry is shifting toward AI generation for movies, music, and images, we want Vibe Jungle to be a forerunner that revolutionises the global entertainment industry in Africa,”_ [said](https://magazine.thenextafrica.com/vibe-jungle-secures-400000-to-make-music-and-film-production-more-affordable-for-african-creators/) Keziah Aboribo, chief executive officer, Vibe Jungle.

Its production arm, Vibe Jungle Studios, creates short films and music videos using AI-assisted tools built on API infrastructure from ByteDance. The company also manages an AI artist.

The $400k will go towards three areas: an AI studio offering video and image tools for artists, filmmakers and brands; original content production through Vibe Jungle Studios covering films, series and music; and a music catalogue that artists can license and build on.

The company is also developing an AI-powered entertainment platform with subscription plans aimed at reducing costs for independent creators. The platform is currently in internal testing ahead of a public launch.

The company plans to expand into interactive gaming and licensing its AI voices and digital images. This is part of its goal to develop Vibe Jungle into a complete creative studio.
