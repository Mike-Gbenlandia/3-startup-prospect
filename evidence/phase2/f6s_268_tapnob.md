## About Tapnob

Tapnob is a payments and settlement platform that connects Bitcoin and stablecoins to the money people and businesses already use in Africa.

• Tapnob for Merchants: online checkout, payment links and QR payments in Bitcoin or stablecoins.

• Tapnob Trade: an OTC desk for large trades and cross-border business payments. It already handles settlement for a global food delivery platform.
