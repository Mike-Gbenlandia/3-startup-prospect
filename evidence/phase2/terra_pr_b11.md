# Terra Industries Raises Additional $18M in Funding

**[Terra Industries](https://www.terraindustries.co/)**, a Danville, CA-based provider of autonomous security systems for critical infrastructure, raised an additional $18M in financing.

The round, which closed its seed funding at $52M, included participation from 8VC, Silent Ventures, Nova Global, Belief Capital, SV Angel, Norleo Space Investments, and Grant Gordon.

The company intends to use the funds to open its first international office in London, expand manufacturing capacity, accelerate deployments across the Global South, and grow its engineering, operations, and business development teams.

Founded in 2024 by CEO Nathan Nwachuku and Maxwell Maduka, Terra Industries provides autonomous defense systems that enable governments and infrastructure operators to monitor and secure critical assets across land, air, and maritime environments. Their platform integrates long- and mid-range autonomous drones, interceptor drones, sentry towers, and unmanned ground vehicles, all connected through ArtemisOS, a proprietary software platform that enables real-time threat detection, autonomous mission planning, and coordinated response.

They currently protect power plants, mines, and other infrastructure assets valued at approximately $11B across multiple African countries
