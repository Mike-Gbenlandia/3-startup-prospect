**Women-focused accelerator Her Startup has selected 12 tech startups from seven African countries for its second cohort.**

Her Startup is backed by KSH Foundation, Tomorrow Foundation and EHA Impact Ventures, and is this year running the programme with Founder Institute Morocco.

After receiving 468 applications from 33 countries, more than double the number for its first edition, 20 founders were shortlisted, and the accelerator increased its intake from 10 to 12. The selected startups get Founder Institute training, startup capital through convertible revenue-based financing, and six months of mentorship.

Half the cohort comes from Nigeria, namely Kadosh Mechatronics, which makes solar-powered greenhouse sensors; FriendnPal, an AI-powered mental health platform; Glorious Farmgate, a poultry automation startup; Fuse Varsity, an ed-tech company; Odnaworld, an agrifood firm; and TESA Global Solutions, a media-tech company.

The other six startups are SAB Biomanufactures (Tanzania), which turns brown seaweed into products for agriculture and food preservation; Uptech Africa (Malawi), an agri-tech marketplace; AEN Holdings (Uganda), which repurposes used batteries; EON (Ethiopia), a plastics recycler; Dosy (Egypt), a micromobility platform for women; and UNTUM Recycling (South Africa).

“The selected founders can look forward to an intensive and transformative journey combining mentorship, practical insights, expert guidance and access to a valuable entrepreneurial network,” said Youssef Koun and Hicham Sentissi, who lead the Founder Institute Morocco chapter.
