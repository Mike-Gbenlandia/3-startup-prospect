## About Jenzy

Jenzy builds cross-border payment infrastructure for African trade, serving importers, exporters, fintechs and payment providers. Its platform routes each transaction across banking networks, mobile money operators and regulated stablecoins, switching rails when one fails. Licensed partners hold customer funds, and the service covers twelve African currencies paired with reserve currencies. It was founded by Bongani Malaba, Eniola Ajuwon, Daniel Kinuthia and Faisal Patel.
