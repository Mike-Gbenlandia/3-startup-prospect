## About Boundlessfi

Boundless is a Web3 funding and collaboration platform built on the Stellar blockchain, created to fix what’s broken in how ideas get funded. Too often, promising projects fail not because of lack of vision, but because of mistrust, poor accountability, and inefficient coordination between builders and funders. Boundless changes that by making funding transparent, programmable, and results-driven from day one.
