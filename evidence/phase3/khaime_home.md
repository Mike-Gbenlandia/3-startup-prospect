## Questions sellers ask

Can't find your answer? Email support@khaime.ai and we'll help.

What is Khaime?

Khaime is an online store and payments platform for businesses anywhere in the world. You set your prices in your own currency, buyers pay in theirs, and you get exactly the price you set. Khaime gives you an AI-built online store, payment links and invoices, Khai (an AI sales assistant that replies to customers and follows up on carts they leave behind), affiliate, coupon and rewards tools, and a mobile app for iPhone and Android. Businesses that already have a website can add Khaime checkout with a WordPress plugin or the Khaime API.

Who can use Khaime?

Any business that sells online, wherever it is based. You can sell to buyers in your own country and abroad from the same store.

How do buyers in other countries pay me?

They see your prices in their own currency and pay in it, by card or another payment method available where they are. Khaime handles the conversion, and you get the price you set in your currency.

How do I get my money?

Payments go into your Khaime wallet. From your dashboard or the Khaime app, you can request a withdrawal to your bank account.

What does Khai do?

Khai is Khaime's AI sales assistant. When you turn it on, it replies to your customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger and follows up on carts they left behind, in your business's name and within the settings you choose.

Do I need my own WhatsApp Business number for Khai?

Not if your store is in Nigeria or Ghana. Khai can answer your customers from Khaime's own WhatsApp number, and every message shows your store's name. You can still connect your own WhatsApp Business number later. Stores in other countries connect their own number.

Do I need a website already?

No. Tell Khaime what you sell and it builds your store for you. If you already have a website, you can add Khaime checkout to it instead.

How much does Khaime cost?

Khaime is free to start. Paid plans add more features as you grow. See the pricing page for details.
